Refunds & Cancellations

What happens if a booking ends early.

The short version

Deposits are non-refundable. Everything you have paid beyond the deposit for work not yet done is refunded.

Why deposits are not returned

A deposit holds a date and turns other work away. Once it is paid that capacity is gone whether or not the project runs, which is the cost it covers.

If you cancel mid-project

Tell us in writing at matthew@mccluster.org. We stop, account for the work completed to that point, and refund the unearned balance on a pro-rata basis. You receive the work produced up to the point of cancellation.

If we cancel

If McCluster Corp cannot continue for any reason of its own, you are refunded in full, deposit included.

Digital goods and licences

Recorded music and downloadable files are refundable only if they were not delivered or the file is faulty. Ask and we will fix or refund it.

Donations

Donations buy nothing and are not refundable. If one was made in error, write within 30 days and we will return it.

How refunds are paid

To the original payment method, through Stripe. We start it within 5 business days of agreeing it; your bank decides how long it takes to appear.

Client sites we built

If you paid a business through a website McCluster built, your contract is with that business and their refund policy applies, not this one. Contact them first. If you cannot reach them, write to us and we will help you find them.

Disputes

Please write to matthew@mccluster.org before raising a chargeback. Almost everything is faster to fix directly.
