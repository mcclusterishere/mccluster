Case study · infrastructure

Building out a 5,000 sq ft data center

Infrastructure Associate, through PeopleSERVE · IPC Systems · Fairfield, Connecticut · 2021 to 2023

From 2021 to 2023 Matthew McCluster worked on the transformation of a 5,000 square foot data center at IPC Systems in Fairfield, Connecticut, as part of the staff PeopleSERVE supplied to the project. The work was physical and hands-on: building out network and server racks, running copper and fiber, installing and configuring power distribution and environmental monitoring, racking Cisco switching, configuring DHCP scopes, and installing Dell, Oracle and Sun servers on Windows Server, Red Hat Enterprise Linux and CentOS. IPC's own description of the engagement is linked below as the primary evidence.

What this experience is evidence of

  • Data center technician work: rack and stack, cable management and labeling, copper and fiber, power distribution and environmental monitoring in a live facility.
  • Network hardware: Cisco Nexus 9000-series and 4500-series switching hardware and DHCP scope configuration.
  • Server hardware and operating systems: Dell, Oracle and Sun servers; Windows Server, Red Hat Enterprise Linux and CentOS; RAID on PERC controllers.
  • Vendor hardware: IPC trading-turret equipment (Touch, Pulse, IQMax and Edge) in IPC's performance test center: installation, refurbishment and firmware updates.
  • Secure decommissioning: DoD-standard media wiping and ISO-standard e-waste handling.
