IT, infrastructure & platform engineering

Matthew McCluster · IT support · data-center infrastructure · platform engineering · Connecticut

Matthew McCluster has worked in IT since 2018. He spent three years in desktop and IT support at Robert Half in Norwalk, Connecticut; worked on the transformation of a 5,000 sq ft data center at IPC Systems in Fairfield, Connecticut from 2021 to 2023; and since 2025 has designed and operated the platform behind McCluster Corp, the organization he founded: a PostgreSQL database with row-level security, serverless APIs on Cloudflare Workers, payments, Linux services, and the release automation that ships it. He holds CompTIA ITF+ and a CT State Community College IT Bootcamp certificate, is studying computer science at Southern Connecticut State University, and is available for on-site work across Connecticut and New York.
