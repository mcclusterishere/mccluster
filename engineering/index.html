Matthew McCluster · IT support & systems · data center & networking · platform & backend · field technology & telematics

Matthew McCluster has worked in IT since 2018. He spent three years in desktop and IT support at Robert Half in Norwalk, Connecticut; worked on the transformation of a 5,000 sq ft data center at IPC Systems in Fairfield, Connecticut from 2021 to 2023; and since 2025 has designed and operated the platform behind McCluster Corp, the organization he founded: a PostgreSQL database with row-level security, serverless APIs on Cloudflare Workers, payments, Linux services, and the release automation that ships it. Through Whip Equipped LLC he works with GPS and fleet telematics and vehicle electrical and embedded diagnostics. He holds CompTIA ITF+ and a CT State Community College IT Bootcamp certificate, is a first-year Computer Science student at Southern Connecticut State University, and is available for on-site work across Connecticut and New York.
