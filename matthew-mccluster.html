Matthew McCluster is a Computer Science student at Southern Connecticut State University and an infrastructure and technical-operations professional working across Connecticut and Georgia. His experience spans desktop support, enterprise data-center and network/server work, PostgreSQL and serverless platform operations, and automotive telematics. He founded McCluster Corp and operates its technology, civic, education and creative projects, including Equity Uprise, PRIM3 and the Uprise Action Network. Through Whip Equipped LLC, he is developing connected-mobility and vehicle systems around GNSS/telematics, sensors and an in-development urban motorcycle platform. He also works in photography, web design, media production and songwriting.
