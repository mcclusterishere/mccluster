Matthew McCluster is an infrastructure engineer, platform builder and creative director working across Connecticut and Georgia. He spent five years in enterprise IT and infrastructure — data-center buildout, Cisco networking, Windows and Linux server estates, and endpoint support at IPC Systems and Robert Half — and now designs and operates the platform behind McCluster Corp: Postgres schema and row-level security, serverless APIs, payments, and an automated release gate. He founded its civic fellowship Equity Uprise, work recognized in 2025 by the State of Georgia and the City of Bridgeport with official proclamations, and owns Whip Equipped LLC, a parts-licensed automotive venture he organized in Acworth, Georgia in 2024. He is also a photographer, web designer and songwriter, recording under the Here / PRIM3 catalogue, and the architect of the Street Credit Bureau reputation platform.
