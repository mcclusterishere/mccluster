# Splat captures

Empty. Volumetric captures live here as `.sog` or `.spz` once they exist.

**These are not files you can write.** A 3D Gaussian Splat is trained from
real footage of a real object — see `docs/spatial-commerce.md` for how to
shoot and convert one. Nothing in this directory can be authored by hand
or produced by a model, which is exactly why the manifest ships empty
instead of pointing at a stand-in.

Budget: keep a product capture under ~8 MB transferred. The customer is a
student on a phone on campus wifi who wants a shake in under a minute.
