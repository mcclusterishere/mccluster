Pseudo-shader contract:

base = sample(baseColor)
normal = sample(normal)
rough = sample(roughness)
metal = sample(metallic)
ao = sample(ao)
brush = sample(brushMask)
grazing = sample(grazingLightMask)
rubyReflect = sample(rubyReflectionMask)

surface = PBR(base, normal, rough, metal, ao)
surface += environmentReflection * grazing * brushResponse
surface += rubyColor * rubyReflect * rubyReflectionIntensity

Rules:
- black steel should look black until light reveals it
- the reflection moves; the metal surface does not pulse
- preserve media dominance
- use ruby reflection only for semantic active states
- on low-power/mobile fallback, static texture + one subtle CSS light sweep is enough
