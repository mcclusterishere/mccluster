Pseudo-shader contract:

glass = dielectricPBR(
  baseColor = sample(baseColor),
  normal = sample(normal),
  roughness = sample(roughness),
  ior = 1.50,
  transmission = sample(transmissionMask),
  thickness = sample(thicknessMask)
)

glass += environmentReflection * sample(reflectionMask) * viewAngleResponse
glass += rubyEnvironment * sample(rubyReflectionMask) * semanticRubyReflection
backgroundBlur *= sample(backdropBlurMask)
opticalDensity *= adaptiveReadability(mediaLuminance, sample(readabilityMask))

Rules:
- smoked glass is a separation layer, not the page aesthetic
- dynamically protect text readability over bright video/photo
- ruby is reflected light, not internal emission
- no cyan tint
- no universal frosted cards
- preserve existing DOM geometry and interactions
