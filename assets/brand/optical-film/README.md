# HERE Optical Film v1

Asset 6 and final material in HERE Material System 2.0 — Production Skin.

This is the missing bridge between the physical material system and the actual content
that makes HERE valuable: photography, video, album artwork, 360 media and portfolio work.

The goal is NOT to put media inside metal boxes.

Instead, the existing media receives a nearly invisible optical layer that can respond to
viewing angle and virtual lighting with restrained reflection, edge refraction, tiny
chromatic dispersion, lens flare and depth.

## Critical rule

The original image/video is always dominant.

Do not crop it differently, recolor it, obscure it, restructure it, or slow it down just
to show this material.
