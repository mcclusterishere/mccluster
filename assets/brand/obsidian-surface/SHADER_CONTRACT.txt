Pseudo-shader contract:

base = sample(baseColor)
n = sample(normal)
r = sample(roughness)
ao = sample(ao)
scrollMask = sample(scrollLightMask)
rubyMask = sample(rubyVeinMask)

surface = PBR(base, n, roughness=r, metalness=0, ao=ao)
surface += environmentLight * scrollMask * lightAngleResponse
surface += rubyColor * rubyMask * rubyIntensity

Rules:
- do not modulate the full baseColor with pulse/sine animation
- rubyIntensity should be event/scroll-state driven
- lightAngleResponse should change with virtual light/camera movement
- keep rubyIntensity at 0 for ordinary resting surfaces
