# Creator 3-day trial, dynamic peer pool and 90-day selection
Status: product design and offline scoring prototype; not deployed or wired to automated ranking.

## Three pillars (equal weights)
- Market impact (33⅓%): consented verified qualified traffic, engagement, attributed signups, activated members, music listens/saves and cross-artist discovery. Normalize by creator stage, opportunity, measured days, and source quality; do not use raw follower count as score.
- Creative talent (33⅓%): role-specific portfolio rubric evaluated by at least two independent trained reviewers. Evidence: technique, originality, craft, audience fit and demonstrated capability. Calibrate raters and permit appeals; do not use inferred protected traits or engagement as proxy for talent.
- Execution and commercial readiness (33⅓%): publishes at least one actionable service, follows a realistic work plan, responds to feedback, improves profile conversion, and documents service fulfillment readiness. Accommodate creator capacity and accessibility needs.

Score each pillar 0–100; average. Missing evidence is 'pending review', never zero. Human panel makes final selection. No automatic rejections.

## Dynamic reference pool
- Creator enters upon free profile signup; only enters scored comparison after 3 fully observed consented days and sufficient data.
- Compare within discipline, experience/stage and cohort window; pool minimum 20 verified comparable creators. Small cohorts use broader clearly labeled pools or 'insufficient peers'.
- Peer percentile uses midrank ties and is reported separately from absolute growth.
- Maintain immutable score snapshots with version, observation window, source provenance, peer group and sample size. Refresh live rankings without rewriting old report cards.
- Exclude bot/fraud traffic and unverified signups; quality weighting must be documented and auditable. Provide corrections/appeals.

## Onboarding: 3 days as a preview of the selected 90-day program
Day 0: explain consent and attribution; create free landing page and one service; select discipline, starting stage, available weekly hours, goals, and portfolio examples; teach how to share tracked profile URL and verify analytics.
Day 1: publish and share first content; learn traffic sources, unique visitors, engagement quality, and why views are not customers.
Day 2: refine service offer and CTA; inspect qualified listeners, saves, signups, cross-artist discovery, and inquiries; avoid artificial engagement.
Day 3: review dashboard and portfolio; complete an execution task and receive provisional report card with evidence gaps.
Day 4: one complimentary 15-minute strategy consultation subject to actual booking availability. Discuss reach, content, craft, pricing and next steps. Creator gets a personalized 14-day plan and 28-day checkpoint.
If selected: 90-day structured coaching, campaign participation, content and service optimization, accountability reviews and milestone recalibration; describe actual included services, availability, eligibility and any costs before acceptance. Do not promise admission.

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] Instrument verified events and referral attribution; missing-data state distinct from zero.
- [ ] Implement pool assignment and server-calculated percentile with minimum group size.
- [ ] Create role-specific portfolio submission and two-reviewer talent rubric with appeal.
- [ ] Persist versioned pillar scores, evidence references, peer cohort snapshots and selection rationale.
- [ ] Build guided onboarding screens with completion progress, Day 1–3 learning tasks and 90-day program disclosure.
- [ ] One consultation entitlement and real calendar booking.
- [ ] Secure owner-only results; no peer-identifying metrics in creator dashboard.
- [ ] Validate scoring for gaming, small cohorts, accessibility, disparate outcomes and reviewer consistency.
