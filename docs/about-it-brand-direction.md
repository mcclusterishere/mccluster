# ABOUT IT — proposed brand direction

Status: **brand concept only; trademark, slogans, and domain are not cleared.** Do not rename production properties, repositories, URLs, or legal entities until clearance and approval.

## Brand language

- **ABOUT IT.** — proposed public-facing platform name.
- **We're About It.** — **community message**, the collective identity of the network.
- **I'm About It.** — **individual participant declaration**, an affirmative choice to participate.
- **Are you about it?** / **You about it?** — invitation and discovery prompt, deliberately conversational and a little mysterious.
- **If you know, you know.** — optional cultural tone, not a substitute for product clarity.
- **Act Now** — contextual call-to-action for a specific mission, not the proposed platform name.
- **Where About It?** — exploratory discovery phrasing, not an approved name or navigation label.

## Experience narrative

1. Someone encounters a creator, campaign, or invitation: **You about it?**
2. They can see immediately what the creator is doing, what participation entails, and what happens next. The brand can carry intrigue; task flows must be explicit.
3. They opt in: **I'm About It.** The declaration must correspond to a real, informed action, never imply consent to unrelated terms or commitments.
4. Participation connects them to others: **We're About It.**
5. Relevant, specific missions offer **Act Now**, with clear task, eligibility, rewards, and consequences.

## Creator-first principles

- A creator's own name, art, audience, and campaigns lead their site. Avoid framing their audience as belonging to McCluster or to a single platform owner.
- Site creation and private preview should be available without Stripe Connect onboarding; payout onboarding is separate.
- Public publishing eligibility is based on qualifying creator subscription **or** approved selected-cohort participation, subject to server-side verification. This is product intent, not a claim that the full enforcement flow is deployed.
- Keep attribution to the operating infrastructure discreet where appropriate; do not obscure legal, payment, or privacy disclosures.
- No dark patterns: joining a campaign, joining a community, paying, and connecting a payout account are separate explicit choices.

## Voice and creative direction

Confident, concise, culturally natural, and participatory. More like a conversation between peers than corporate marketing. Let people discover what 'it' means through creators and real action. Avoid generic startup jargon and forced explanation in campaign headlines. Support plain-language accessibility in instructions and transactional interfaces.

## Brand/legal release gate

Before using ABOUT IT, We're About It, I'm About It, or related phrases as commercial marks, obtain current USPTO and common-law similarity review for software, marketplaces, entertainment, advertising, and community services; check relevant domains and social handles; confirm brand ownership and usage strategy. Earlier preliminary searches did **not** establish clearance. Keep existing Action Network references intact until a separately reviewed rollout.
