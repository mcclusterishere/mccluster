# Founder 40 — launch acquisition experiment

Status: planned, not launched. Founder 40 is McCluster's initial creator program. Founder 20 is a **subsequent experimental variant**, not a product, plan or subscription tier.

## Cohort and deliverables

Five roles: recording artists, producers, audio engineers, videographers and video editors. Aim for 40 selected participants, with an initial planning assumption of eight per role; adjust based on actual production dependencies and geography. Artists, engineers and videographers should be locally compatible for in-person production; producers and editors can be remote. An artist completes one song; an engineer records one artist's song; a videographer shoots one video; an editor edits one deliverable for a videographer; producers contribute one production. Specify acceptance criteria and collaborator rights before work begins.

## Initial acquisition test (assumption: $20 per role, not per applicant)

| Role | Paid ads | Recruiting/job platforms | Total |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Recording artists | $10 | $10 | $20 |
| Producers | $10 | $10 | $20 |
| Audio engineers | $10 | $10 | $20 |
| Videographers | $10 | $10 | $20 |
| Video editors | $10 | $10 | $20 |
| **Total** | **$50** | **$50** | **$100** |

These are test allocations only, not estimates of 40 hires or 500 applications. If the user's $20 meant **per recruited participant**, the budget would instead be $800 for 40 people; confirm before spending.

## Recruitment channels to verify before launch

- Paid social: test creator-specific creative and targeting, using one UTM campaign per role. Check actual minimum spend and conversion tracking before placing ads.
- Job and recruitment platforms: verify whether listings for paid project-based creative work are permitted, available at the intended price, and accessible in each recruitment country.
- ProFellow: verify suitability and listing requirements; do not assume it is a general job board or offers $10 listings.
- Direct campus/community/creator referrals: track separately from paid channels, obtain consent and never claim a placement is paid unless compensation is authorized.

Do not purchase ads or job listings without explicit approval and a working application funnel.

## Funnel instrumentation

For each role and channel record: landing views, application starts, submitted applications, verified unique accounts, portfolio-qualified candidates, contacted candidates, accepted placements, completed assignments, approved deliverables, and total actual spend. Report CPA per qualified applicant, cost per accepted creator, cost per approved deliverable, time to completion and collaborator satisfaction. Avoid counting duplicate applications as unique recruits.

## Experimental design

1. Founder 40 is the baseline: lock eligibility, recruitment channels, quality rubric, compensation and geography before running.
2. Run the campaign, observe production outcomes and record drop-off reasons.
3. Founder 20 is a later smaller-cohort variant. Compare outcomes normalized by participant and dollars spent, not just raw totals. Preserve role mix, recruitment conditions and eligibility as far as practical; otherwise explicitly note confounding.
4. Continue only if smaller-cohort outcomes show a meaningful operational advantage, not merely fewer total costs.

## Operational safeguards

Cohort selection is separate from account membership, website subscriptions and identity verification. Participants receive written scopes, payment terms, rights and publication consent. Client-funded campaigns cannot promise or trigger worker compensation until funding is verified. Recruitment analytics and contact details must be private and tenant-scoped. The $7.50 monthly / $60 annual creator subscription is a separate product and does not fund this cohort.

## Before spending

Confirm whether $20 is **per role** or **per acquired person**, exact recruiting geographies, compensation terms for selected participants, working intake/backend and analytics attribution, and channel-specific pricing and posting rules.
