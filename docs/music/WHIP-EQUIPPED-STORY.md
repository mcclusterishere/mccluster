# Whip Equipped — the story (canon)

**Canonical storyline for the Whip Equipped album and film.** Owner, 2026-10-31: “This is a light idea of how the movie Whip Equipped should go. Find where in the repo there's a storyline about Whip Equipped and put it over there. If not, just make this the canon.” No earlier storyline existed in the repository, so this is the canon.

- **Fiction.** Everything below is a screen and song story: characters (McCluster, Rico, Majid, the love interest, the lil homies), crimes, deals and violence are invented for the film and the album. Nothing here describes real people or real events.
- **Whose project.** Whip Equipped (the album and film) is Matthew McCluster's music project, recorded as McCluster and published on his property, `https://matthew.mccluster.org` (`data/albums.json` → `whip-equipped`). It shares its name with **Whip Equipped LLC, Matthew's separate company**, which is not a McCluster Corp brand and is not this album (`CLAUDE.md` rule 13, `docs/control-plane/DOMAINS-AND-ENTITIES.md`).
- **Lyrics.** Where the owner wrote lyrics below (and in the separate lyric sheets), they are kept as plain text in `data/lyrics/src/<slug>.txt` and timed to the audio by the lyric machine (`core/src/lyrics/`, `tools/lyrics/transcribe.mjs`) into `data/lyrics/<slug>.json`. For Hundun, Salvage Title, Stand On Business and All in the Details the owner's dedicated `.docx` sheets were used; where they differ from this document (Salvage Title's second hook opens “…I tried to rebuild you.” in the sheet), the sheet won.
- **Audio.** The owner's original attachment links pointed at files outside the repository; each is replaced below by the repo track it is, or marked “not yet in the repo”.
