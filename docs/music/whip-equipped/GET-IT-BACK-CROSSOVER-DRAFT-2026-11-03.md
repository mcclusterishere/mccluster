# Before the song

## 1. A pair for work

*INT. FICTIONAL DESIGNER KICKS STOREFRONT — BRIDGEPORT — DAY.*

*The game adaptation at Jimmy's location. The showroom and its staff are wholly fictional. This scene does not portray the real retailer's interior, manufacturing, employees, ownership, or conduct.*

*The STORE FITTER sets a box beside McCluster. Its model label faces away. An adult CREW MEMBER waits at the end of the fitting bench, wearing commercial Robbery 1s replicas.*

**FITTER:** You called about these?

**MCCLUSTER:** I did. Sorry it took me a minute.

**CREW MEMBER:** A minute was the first estimate.

**MCCLUSTER:** You got somewhere else to be?

**CREW MEMBER:** Apparently wherever your phone says.

*McCluster's phone lights up. He looks at it, then turns it over on the bench.*

**FITTER:** Both shoes. Walk around before you decide.

**MCCLUSTER:** I already know I like them.

**FITTER:** That's half the appointment.

*McCluster changes shoes. He takes a short walk between the displays. He slows at his reflection, then sees the crew member watching.*

**MCCLUSTER:** I'm checking the fit.

**CREW MEMBER:** Of course.

*He walks back, deliberately ordinary this time.*

**MCCLUSTER:** These work.

**FITTER:** Wearing them out?

*His phone lights again. McCluster reads the message. The overdue rental has still not come back.*

**MCCLUSTER:** Yeah. I've got a car to go get.

**CREW MEMBER:** You said that was getting dropped off.

**MCCLUSTER:** It was supposed to be.

**CREW MEMBER:** When?

**MCCLUSTER:** Earlier.

*The fitter waits with the empty box. McCluster notices he has made everyone in the room wait for the answer he does not want to give.*

**MCCLUSTER:** Yesterday. They said yesterday, then this morning. Now nobody's answering.

**CREW MEMBER:** Do we know where it is?

**MCCLUSTER:** Somebody thinks they saw it. We'll look. Then I need to get back to the office.

*The crew member lifts one foot clear of the bench leg to avoid rubbing the replica against it. McCluster notices.*

**MCCLUSTER:** You bought the Robbery Ones?

**CREW MEMBER:** Replicas. June checked the label with me like there was going to be a test.

**MCCLUSTER:** Was there?

**CREW MEMBER:** She asked if I understood the return policy. I got that one wrong.

*McCluster laughs. The fitter puts his old shoes in the new box and hands him a receipt.*

**FITTER:** Keep this.

**MCCLUSTER:** They're for work. They're staying on.

**FITTER:** Then you'll know where you wore them first.

*He folds the receipt into his wallet. The crew member gets the door. Both leave on foot toward their parked work vehicle. The shoes have no sound cue or effect beyond ordinary footsteps.*

## 2. Almost the right car

*EXT. COMPOSITE CONNECTICUT STREET — LATER.*

*A light-gray sedan stands at the curb. It resembles the missing rental. The street is invented; no private address, real plate, or real vehicle identifier is shown.*

*McCluster stops a few feet from it. The crew member stays beside him.*

**CREW MEMBER:** That it?

**MCCLUSTER:** Same color. Same shape.

**CREW MEMBER:** That's two things.

*McCluster looks at the car again. His relief arrived before the evidence did.*

**MCCLUSTER:** It looks like it.

*A CAR OWNER, an adult composite character, comes from a nearby doorway carrying groceries.*

**CAR OWNER:** Can I help you?

**MCCLUSTER:** We're looking for a rental that wasn't brought back. We thought this might be it.

**CAR OWNER:** This is my car.

*McCluster looks from the owner to the sedan. The answer interrupts the version of the afternoon he had already started telling himself.*

**MCCLUSTER:** All right. We could have the wrong one.

**CAR OWNER:** You do.

*The owner sets one grocery bag down to free a hand.*

**MCCLUSTER:** Would you be comfortable checking the vehicle number against the one we've got? You don't have to hand us anything.

*The owner considers him, then agrees to compare it without surrendering the keys or documents. The camera stays on faces and grocery bags. No identifier or checking procedure is displayed.*

*A moment later, the owner shows the mismatch. McCluster checks his own reference again.*

**MCCLUSTER:** It doesn't match.

**CAR OWNER:** No.

**MCCLUSTER:** I'm sorry. We were wrong.

**CAR OWNER:** Then I need you to move. I've got food going warm.

*They step away immediately. The crew member catches a replica's edge against the curb. An ordinary scuff, with no stumble or supernatural suggestion.*

*McCluster starts to reach for the bag, intending to help.*

**CAR OWNER:** I've got it.

*He stops. The owner loads the groceries and gets into the car. The sedan leaves under its owner's control. Neither man touches, enters, blocks, follows, or takes it.*

*The crew member looks down at the shoe, then back at McCluster.*

**CREW MEMBER:** We almost had a very different afternoon.

**MCCLUSTER:** We were wrong about a car. We checked it.

**CREW MEMBER:** You'd already stopped looking for the differences.

*McCluster puts the reference away.*

**MCCLUSTER:** Yeah.

*His phone rings. DESK LEAD appears on the screen.*

**MCCLUSTER — PHONE:** It wasn't ours.

**DESK LEAD — PHONE:** You saw the number?

**MCCLUSTER:** Doesn't match. Owner's here. We left them alone.

**DESK LEAD — PHONE:** All right. I've had two more people ask when you'll be back.

**MCCLUSTER:** Give me a little longer.

**DESK LEAD — PHONE:** How long?

*He looks along the street. There is no new lead waiting at the end of it.*

**MCCLUSTER:** I don't know yet.

**DESK LEAD — PHONE:** Then that's what I'll tell them.

*The call ends. McCluster folds the reference without looking at it again. The crew member puts both feet on the ground and waits for him to move.*

**MCCLUSTER:** Let's go.

*The last spoken line ends before the future recording slot.*

# After the song

## 1. What came back

*INT. COMPOSITE WHIP WORK OFFICE — NIGHT.*

*McCluster sets his old-shoe box beneath a chair. The crew member remains by the door. The desk lead has kept one lamp on.*

**DESK LEAD:** Nothing?

**MCCLUSTER:** Nothing we can bring back.

**DESK LEAD:** Sit down a minute.

*McCluster does not.*

**MCCLUSTER:** Somebody answer?

**DESK LEAD:** A message came in. It says the rental may have been involved in a crash.

*The crew member comes a little farther into the room.*

**MCCLUSTER:** Where is it?

**DESK LEAD:** We don't have a confirmed location.

**MCCLUSTER:** Is it our car?

**DESK LEAD:** That hasn't been confirmed either.

**MCCLUSTER:** Is anybody hurt?

**DESK LEAD:** The message doesn't tell us that.

*McCluster takes the chair. The desk lead turns the screen enough for him to read. No private message, name, identifier, or real location appears to the audience.*

**MCCLUSTER:** This could be somebody telling us anything.

**DESK LEAD:** It could. I wasn't going to hide it until I had a better answer.

**MCCLUSTER:** I didn't say you should.

*The sharpness lasts a second longer than he wants.*

**MCCLUSTER:** I know. Thank you for telling me.

*He reads again. It does not acquire more detail on the second pass.*

**CREW MEMBER:** You want me to stay?

**MCCLUSTER:** There's nothing to do right now.

**CREW MEMBER:** I asked if you wanted me to stay.

*McCluster moves the empty chair out from the desk with his foot. The crew member sits.*

**DESK LEAD:** What do you want me putting on the return sheet?

**MCCLUSTER:** Missing. Still missing.

**DESK LEAD:** And the crash report?

**MCCLUSTER:** Reported. Not confirmed. Don't let it become confirmed because the next person reads it quickly.

*The desk lead makes the note.*

**DESK LEAD:** There's somebody booked for a vehicle tomorrow. They asked twice whether they'd need another arrangement.

**MCCLUSTER:** I told them we'd have this one back.

**DESK LEAD:** I know.

**MCCLUSTER:** Tell them we don't have it. I'll speak to them in the morning about what we can actually offer.

**DESK LEAD:** Before they leave home.

**MCCLUSTER:** Before they leave home.

*The desk lead puts that beside the return sheet. Two ordinary jobs that now have to be done because one promised return did not happen.*

**CREW MEMBER:** When we saw that car, I thought we were done.

**MCCLUSTER:** So did I.

**CREW MEMBER:** I was thinking about where we could stop to eat.

*McCluster looks toward the shoe box under his chair.*

**MCCLUSTER:** I was already thinking about telling everybody we got it back.

*The crew member lets that stand.*

**MCCLUSTER:** I keep hearing the way that person said it was their car. Like I was the problem they had to get past.

**DESK LEAD:** For a minute, you were.

**MCCLUSTER:** Yeah.

*He unfolds the new-shoe receipt from his wallet. A purchase completed this morning, with a clear beginning and end. He folds it again.*

**CREW MEMBER:** You can still eat.

**MCCLUSTER:** I know.

**CREW MEMBER:** Come on, then. You can be mad at the table.

*McCluster almost smiles. He stands, picks up the old shoes, and looks once more at the unfilled return space through the window.*

**MCCLUSTER:** Leave that light on until you finish.

**DESK LEAD:** I'm nearly done.

*They go. The desk lead completes the note: NO VEHICLE RECOVERED. The crash remains a separate, unconfirmed report. The lamp goes off after the work is finished.*

## 2. The small thing that can be fixed

*INT. ROBBERY 1s EXCHANGE — BRIDGEPORT — ANOTHER DAY.*

*Leo Mercer turns a commercial replica over at the repair counter. The crew member waits. The original-pair display remains empty.*

**LEO:** Caught the curb?

**CREW MEMBER:** Stepping out of somebody's way.

**LEO:** I can repair the edge. You'll still see where it happened.

**CREW MEMBER:** That's fine.

*Samira Diallo passes with copies of the shop's zine. She recognizes the pair.*

**SAMIRA:** That was quick.

**CREW MEMBER:** Long day.

**SAMIRA:** You want to tell me?

**CREW MEMBER:** We went looking for a car. Found the wrong one. Went home without the right one.

**SAMIRA:** Did you find it later?

**CREW MEMBER:** No confirmed news.

*Samira rests the zines on the counter without opening one.*

**SAMIRA:** That's a hard place to leave a story.

**CREW MEMBER:** That's where it is.

*Leo writes a repair ticket. He checks the shoe's replica label before attaching his part of it.*

**LEO:** Just the damaged edge?

**CREW MEMBER:** Just that.

**LEO:** All right. Keep your half.

*The crew member folds the ticket into a wallet. Leo puts the shoe on an ordinary repair shelf. The mark remains visible beside the commercial replica label.*

*The original-pair plinth stays empty.*
