# Format and performance

For each song of duration T, the structure is **before dialogue 2T → full music video T → after dialogue 2T**. Each chapter therefore has a five-times-song runtime target. The aftermath resolves or complicates that chapter and carries a concrete problem to the next chapter. The next opening receives its own full duration; a bridge is never counted twice.

Dialogue stops before the recording starts. The song plays in full without newly written exposition over the vocal, ducking to accommodate new conversations, or an early fade to reach the next scene. The video can be subjective, symbolic, glamorous, threatening, or built from memory while remaining tied to the lyrics. It does not have to repeat the preceding conversation literally.

These are production targets for a performance pass, not a claim that the scenes have been recorded or cut to exact frames. The dialogue is written at approximately 130 spoken words per minute, with actual counts checked independently of speaker labels, directions, and video descriptions. Extra player branches and exploration can add time; they do not replace the required dialogue. Distinct action beats need to be accommodated in rehearsal without cutting the promised dramatic material.

## Before the song

*Target 3:10.4.*

*EXT. BRIDGEPORT — SMALL DEALERSHIP, DUSK.*

*A salesman returns inside. A silver coupe waits with its engine running. Across the street, a much older vehicle contains McCluster's tool cases, folded clothes and a sleeping bag. ALANA notices before he closes its hatch. They have only just met.*

**ALANA:** You keep your whole business in there?

**ALANA:** I meant the blanket. You can just say you are having a difficult month.

**MCCLUSTER:** You were looking through the window before I spoke to you.

**ALANA:** I like the car. I have not decided about the company.

**MCCLUSTER:** Good. You should test both.

*She approaches the coupe. He opens the passenger door, then realizes she wants the driver's seat. He steps aside.*

**ALANA:** He said the price was firm. Then you said something and suddenly we had the keys.

**MCCLUSTER:** I asked why it had been sitting here. He answered a different question. Then I asked whose car it actually was.

**ALANA:** And you liked the answer?

**MCCLUSTER:** I liked knowing he was nervous.

**ALANA:** That does not make it a good purchase.

**MCCLUSTER:** No. It tells you to keep listening. A person can polish a car more easily than a story.

**ALANA:** There it is. You have a little speech for everything.

**MCCLUSTER:** I can be quiet.

**ALANA:** We will test that too. Are we actually buying this?

**MCCLUSTER:** Not tonight.

**ALANA:** Are we bringing it back?

*He looks toward the office. For the first time, his easy rhythm breaks.*

**MCCLUSTER:** I have somewhere else I could take it.

**ALANA:** Then someone will report it stolen. You understand how that sounds when you say it to somebody you just met?

**MCCLUSTER:** I do. You asked me a direct question.

**ALANA:** You also watched me notice where you sleep. Do not turn that into a reason I should feel sorry for you.

**MCCLUSTER:** I am not asking you to. You can walk away. I will still give you my number.

**ALANA:** I already have your number. You wrote it on the back of a business card with somebody else's logo.

**MCCLUSTER:** The printer wanted payment before delivery.

**ALANA:** Sensible man.

**ALANA:** I spent this morning listening to someone explain why I should be grateful for a smaller life. I came out here because I wanted an hour that did not belong to that conversation.

**MCCLUSTER:** Is that a reason to trust me?

**ALANA:** It is a reason to be honest about what I want. I like the car. I like that you noticed me. I also think you are making a terrible decision.

**MCCLUSTER:** You can like me and still say no.

**ALANA:** I know. Stop trying to manage my answer.

*She sits behind the wheel. He remains outside, leaving the choice visible.*

**ALANA:** Get in. But do not tell me this is harmless.

**MCCLUSTER:** I will not.

**ALANA:** And if you touch the wheel while I am driving, you can walk home.
