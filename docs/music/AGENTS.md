## Preserve provenance and scope

- Keep owner-approved continuity distinct from newly written dialogue, staging, and interpretation. A preserved screenplay is fictional dramatic work, not a transcript of private conversations or proof of biographical events.
- Maintain the fiction label and the distinction between the music/screen project and the separate real Whip Equipped LLC.
- Separate narrative sequence, document presentation order, and in-world chronology. An explicitly earlier flashback can appear later in the document. A flight presented outside Vaunt can still precede the narrative ending.
- Separate the approved narrative from the released catalog and implemented runtime. Do not silently reorder catalog data or claim missing media exists merely because the story lists a chapter. Make catalog/runtime changes when they are within the authorized task and grounded in available media.
- Preserve measured-versus-catalog duration provenance, recording versions, and unresolved lyric alignment. Do not silently substitute a same-named or equal-length recording.
- Keep real car-flip recollections and supporting records separate from invented scene details. Label approximate dates and incomplete evidence.
- Use repository-relative source references. Do not copy raw private chat transcripts, private Page links, or private source-access URLs into public creative documentation.

## Verify affected continuity

For a changed fact, inspect the affected scene and its neighboring entrances, exits, callbacks, and aftermath. Check identities, living/dead state, locations, chronology, responsibility, and the song's actual meaning. Search current notes and manifests for contradictory old statements, including copies in summaries and tables.

Preserve the 2T / T / 2T contract where applicable: each before and after scene has its own target, the whole recording plays, and a transition is not counted twice. If dialogue changes materially, recount spoken words without speaker labels, directions, lyrics, or video prose. If a recording changes, update the related scene targets and totals using its stated evidence basis.

Update the stable guide, manifest, decision ledger, timing when affected, and screenplay consistently. Verify only the parts needed to resolve the change's actual continuity or timing risk. This guide does not require unrelated creative rewrites, new software tests, or another approval step.
