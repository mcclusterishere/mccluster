## 1. Purpose

The album is the flagship attention product and commercial case study.

This protocol asks how an artist-owned web experience can turn outside traffic
into deeper listening, a durable direct relationship, measurable commercial
value, and optional participation in the wider McCluster / Action Network
ecosystem.

The website is not treated merely as a landing page. It is the full product
surface through which acquisition, exposure, listening, return behavior,
identity, support, distribution and downstream commerce can be measured.

## 2. Commercial model

Primary loop:

discovery
→ album/song exposure
→ meaningful listen
→ catalog exploration
→ return visit
→ direct account / relationship
→ support / purchase / licensing / clipping / service / membership
→ retained relationship

The goal is not to maximize raw clicks or time-on-page. The goal is to increase
long-term direct relationship value while maintaining user satisfaction,
catalog diversity, low friction and transparent user control.

## 3. Product questions

1. Which acquisition sources produce durable listeners rather than one-session traffic?
2. Which entry songs create the greatest downstream catalog exploration?
3. Which sequence of album/context/artist/action modules produces the highest return rate?
4. What percentage of listeners become identifiable direct relationships?
5. Which direct relationships generate measurable economic value?
6. Which recommendation and interface policies improve outcomes without collapsing interest diversity?
7. What is the incremental value of keeping the full funnel on an artist-owned property instead of redirecting immediately to a third-party platform?

## 6. Product optimization vs research

### Product optimization

Routine analytics and product experiments may be conducted to operate and improve
the album/site experience under the applicable privacy/product framework.

These results can support business decisions.

### Publication-intent research

If a prospective experiment is designed to contribute to generalizable
knowledge and involves interaction/intervention with living people or
identifiable private information, its human-subjects status must be determined
through the appropriate institutional process before it is represented as
approved/exempt human-subjects research.

The Research Lab database status is a deployment guardrail. It is not itself an
IRB determination.

Historical product traffic is not automatically converted into a research
dataset merely because a paper is later desired.

## 7. Initial hypotheses

These are product hypotheses until a publication-intent protocol is formally
activated.

### H1 — Catalog depth
Visitors who encounter a contextual next-step into a second suitable track will
have greater 7-day catalog breadth than visitors who receive the current
non-adaptive ordering.

### H2 — Direct relationship
Visitors who reach meaningful multi-track listening will create a direct account
at a higher rate than single-track visitors.

### H3 — Return value
Returning listeners will produce greater 30-day commercial value than first-day
conversion attempts alone.

### H4 — Diversity guard
A recommendation policy with an explicit diversity floor will produce similar
or better long-term engagement than a pure highest-score policy while reducing
single-item interest collapse.

### H5 — Artist-owned funnel
Traffic retained inside the direct album experience will create more measurable
first-party relationship events per acquired visitor than traffic immediately
redirected to external platforms.

## 10. Funding translation

The fundable technology is not simply the album website.

The R&D asset is the reusable adaptive-experience and experimentation system:

- opportunity-aware first-party telemetry;
- scientifically grounded audience features;
- stable policy/experiment assignment;
- low-traffic experimentation;
- explicit preference memory;
- research/production policy separation;
- cross-domain recommendation across music, creator, civic/action and commerce;
- constrained adaptive/generative UI;
- reproducible decision/outcome datasets.

The album is the first real commercial testbed and evidence source.

## 11. Decision rule

Do not let new ecosystem projects displace the commercial core unless they
either:

1. materially improve the album/direct-fan funnel;
2. create a reusable research capability;
3. create attributable revenue; or
4. satisfy a separately approved strategic objective.

This protocol is intentionally designed to keep the research system from becoming
another reason not to ship, market and monetize the album.
