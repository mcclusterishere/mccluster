The platform is the product; the free builder is an entry door. The user wants different modules purchasable by different users, with a separately paid domain and one month of Anti-Social/platform access for audience/social/shop use through that domain. Existing domain, tenant, identity, billing, storefront and social engines remain authoritative. ## Explicit expiry policy and domain purchase priority

Customers retain control of their site and existing data after trial expiry. Advanced/white-label modules become visibly unavailable and server-authorized operations must deny unentitled use. Domain registration continues for its purchased annual term independently of the month-long platform trial. Do not infer indefinite hosting funding, automatic renewal or a free domain.

The actual purchase journey is now a priority: existing provider availability/exact annual quote and renewal disclosure → verified payment → registrar-confirmed registration → customer ownership → DNS/TLS/site binding → durable receipt/status and retries → failure/refund recovery → renewal/transfer controls.
