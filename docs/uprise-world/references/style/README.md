# ⛔ ART STYLE ONLY — NOT A CHARACTER REFERENCE ⛔

## Read this before you look at the image in this directory.

`living-sketch-style-reference-board.jpg` contains drawings of a person.

**That person is not Matthew McCluster. That person is not the protagonist.
That person is not any character in Uprise World.**

The figures in STYLE REF 01, 02, 03, and 04 are **anonymous stand-in bodies**.
They exist for one reason and one reason only: to show **how the ink, the
graphite, the wash, and the paper are supposed to look**.

## What this board is authoritative for

**Rendering technique. Nothing else.**

- ink line quality — loose, variable weight, overdrawn, imperfect
- graphite construction marks left visible
- watercolor / marker wash behavior and imperfect coverage
- charcoal-gray grounding
- red editorial scribble accents
- white paper breaking through, negative space preserved
- unfinished, human, hand-drawn edges — never a post-process filter

## What this board is NOT authoritative for

**Anything about who a character is or what they look like.** Specifically, do
not take any of the following from this board, for the protagonist or for any
NPC:

- ❌ face, facial structure, or likeness
- ❌ hair — length, texture, style, locs, or color
- ❌ skin tone
- ❌ beard or facial hair
- ❌ build, height, or body proportions
- ❌ age
- ❌ clothing, garments, colorways, or logos
- ❌ pose, silhouette, or attitude as character design
- ❌ identity of any kind

The blue jacket in STYLE REF 01/03 is **not** the canonical hoodie. The black
tee in STYLE REF 02/04 is **not** canonical wardrobe. The hair in every panel is
**not** the protagonist's hair. None of it carries over. It is a demonstration
of brushwork that happens to have been drawn on top of a human shape.
