# Story authoring

Read [the index](README.md), then the work's `CANON.md`, `REVEAL-MATRIX.md` and `SOURCES.md` before changing its story. Current owner decisions override older manuscript assumptions; unresolved proposals remain labeled proposals. Preserve original snapshots and distinguish a story rewrite from a source import.

These are fictional stories, including fictional versions of people and organizations. Do not treat their relationships or events as claims about real operations. Never turn fictional cyber activity into operational instructions.

Private manuscript text is local-only in this checkpoint. The website excludes `docs/`, but the repository itself is public. Do not publish the manuscript, reveal matrix, source snapshots or spoilers through GitHub, public page data, AI discovery files, or a build artifact without explicit publication authorization. Keep public episode descriptions and private canon separate.
