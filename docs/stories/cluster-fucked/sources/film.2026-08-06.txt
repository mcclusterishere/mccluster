MCCLUSTER CORP
"CLUSTER FUCKED"
A short film. Target 38 minutes including credits.
No script. Beats only. Everybody plays themselves, real names, real you. Read your parts, know the beats, say it however you'd actually say it. Notes are marked plain.
THE 5 RULES — read out loud together before day one
1. Play everything dead serious. Nobody on camera thinks anything is funny. If you laugh, the joke dies.
2. Let silence sit. Don't rush to fill it. The pause IS the joke.
3. The doc camera catches what you're hiding. You get two looks at the lens in the whole film: "you seeing this?" and "you ain't see that." That's it.
4. Whatever's wild, treat it like it's normal. Whatever's normal, treat it like it's wild.
5. If something happens once, it happens three times. Every bit comes back.
CAST

RULES WE DON'T BREAK
- The HR jokes aim at the COMPANY, not at the complaint. The company is too scared to ever check anything — that fear is the punchline, and it's what keeps all three employed. Laire half believes himself every time. Never play it as a full con. The film never rules on it.
- Matthew is never dumb. Not one frame. Smartest man in the building, spending all of it on the wrong thing.
- Tanuj is never scary. Sweetest man on screen. Some of what he says has nothing under it at all — those are the ones that make the other ones work. The scary part arrives after he's gone.
- Blow-ups have no build. Instant. And after, Laire is fine and the ROOM is wrecked.
- The masked man never speaks. He only nods. Nobody in the film finds him strange. That's the joke — not the mask, the fact that nobody's asking.
- The clipboard gets signed by everybody and explained to nobody.
- Every joke has to land in one move, from a cold start, with nothing to remember and nothing to already know. If it needs a running start, it's not a joke yet.
- Not every talking head ends on a beat and a reveal. Some end on an action. Some cut off mid-sentence. Some just stop.
- Things we never show or explain: the raccoon. The seven-fifty. Street Credit Bureau. Who bit the donut. The full clean bodega track. The screening screen. What's on Tanuj's laptop. What's in Katherine's locked drawer. The lottery slip at the security desk. Who Denise is. THE ITCH IS THE PRODUCT.
