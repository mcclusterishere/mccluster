# Mission reboot — live catalog and music alignment (2026-10-13)

## Purpose and constraints
McCluster Corp's public product context defines Equity Uprise as research, civic education, campaigns, culture and community action; the nonprofit also operates educational, creative and technical programs. Missions must deliver a **real benefit**; content is optional secondary documentation, never a condition of assistance. Do not imply that nonprofit donations fund unrelated commercial client work. The three participation paths are **Create / Amplify / Show Up**. Commercial paid clipping retains separate contracts, funding and payout ledger.

## Unified campaign execution
A campaign can contain an approved song, a **Create** deliverable, an **Amplify** clip and a **Show Up** event, all connected to one campaign ID. Track IDs and content IDs are linked by stable UUIDs; never duplicate profiles. Each task has an output, eligibility, proof rubric, consent rules, approval, impact count and attribution. Separate impact metrics (meals delivered, resources verified, events staffed) from reach metrics (plays, clips, views).
