# Action Network — Signed Creator 90-day extension

Status: proposed commercial/workforce program, not active contracts or implemented payouts.

## Selection
After each 90-day cohort, McCluster may sign **one standout creator per cohort** to an additional 90-day term. Define 'cohort' as each five-person production pod; if selecting one per quarterly U.S. and international pod, maximum eight signed creators/year. If the intended selection is one per combined ten-person quarter, maximum four/year. Explicitly decide before budgets and public copy. Selection is not automatic; use a documented rubric: completed approved work, qualified acquisitions (new verified Mnet accounts subscribing to the referring creator), returning subscribers, rights compliance, collaboration quality, reliability and fraud review. Permit appeals and transparent feedback.

## Double reward allocation
Per signed creator/month: $10 upfront + $40 reserved fan rewards + $10 first milestone + $40 final milestone = $100 allocation; maximum direct creator cash $60/month, fan reward budget $40/month. For 90 days: $300 total allocation, $180 maximum creator cash, $120 fan rewards. These figures are proposed budgets and must be funded, contracted, and checked for labor-law compliance.

## Triple challenge intensity
Use threefold *outcome goals*, not unbounded working hours. Proposed signed-creator 90-day stretch objectives: 3,000 qualified new subscribers versus 1,000 original target; up to 36 approved short-form promotional assets versus 12 original, role-adjusted; three founder campaign contributions versus one; 3,000 verified returning subscribers versus 1,000. Do not conflate new accounts, subscriptions, returning users, views or clips. These are stretch goals, not guaranteed compensation triggers; set achievable, role-specific paid milestones with manager review.

## Five role paths
Artist: expanded collaborations and promotional releases, not mandatory triple studio hours.
Producer: three approved beat/collaboration packages with clear rights and stems.
Engineer: expanded approved recording/onboarding targets with studio access and bounded sessions.
Videographer: increased approved footage/campaign coverage with prebooked shoots and production cost budget.
Editor: expanded short-form edits and campaign variations using cleared source footage and revision caps.

## Platform changes
Creator stage state: applicant -> enrolled -> graduated -> selected -> contracted_signed -> active_signed -> completed_signed/terminated. Distinct signed contract and effective dates; role-based advanced curriculum, tougher objective templates, separate funded incentive budget and auditable payment ledger. Keep creator's canonical Mnet account and audience; no account duplication. Signed creator may mentor incoming cohort and take assignments without double-booking; reserve capacity. Role permissions are stage-scoped and tenant-scoped, never inferred from a client-side flag.

## Cost and safety
At eight signed creators/year: up to $2,400 incremental total allocation ($1,440 cash and $960 fan rewards), assuming each serves one 90-day extension. At four/year: $1,200 incremental allocation. These are **additional** to base accelerator and project management budgets, excluding labor, production and legal costs. Validate employment/contractor classification and minimum compensation requirements; $60/month cash may not lawfully compensate required work. Require written terms, rights, cancellation, milestone dispute process, and funded payouts before onboarding.
