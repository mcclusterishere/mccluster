# Action Network Clipper workforce and fan-bounty model

## Role separation
- Clipper: approved contracted creator with campaigns, assignments, payout eligibility, and own fan-bounty campaigns.
- Bounty hunter: fan/participant in a Clipper campaign, not automatically eligible for a Clipper stipend.
- Brand/customer: funds purchased managed program or clipping campaign under separately agreed terms.
- McCluster admin: approves creators, assignments, contracts, payments, and dispute resolution.

## Money movement
- Stipend is compensation, not a loan; track source of funds, creator, contract, amount, period, status, approval, receipt, and disbursement reference.
- Fan bounties are separately funded by the Clipper before publication; reserve funds, cap obligations, verify completion, and prevent unfunded offers.
- Do not automatically deduct a Clipper's bounty spending from their stipend unless the parties explicitly authorize that arrangement and applicable law permits it.
- An off-platform cash advance requires a signed/acknowledged receipt and an administrative ledger entry before marking paid; no invented transfer.
- Example discussed: Heath requests $30. **No payment has been made or recorded.** If authorized, designate $30 as an advance toward his agreed first-month Clipper stipend (not a debt), with agreed period, duties, total stipend, and remaining balance. Do not assume the standard $50/month rate applies to Heath until confirmed.
- Do not conflate twice-monthly payroll with biweekly payroll. Verify classification and local labor/tax requirements before scaling.

## Delivery requirements
- Clipper application intake with secure authenticated storage, consent, admin review, and invitations.
- Role-based Clipper workspace with brief acceptance, media rights, assignments, analytics, and payout statements.
- Fan bounty setup with creator-owned funding source, funding reservation, caps, completion review, payout ledger and abuse prevention.
- Reuse Action Network authentication, org scope, Control work records, analytics, Stripe accounting and existing clipping primitives.
- Never claim automatic payments, fan funding, or production onboarding is live until end-to-end tested.
