Write the change you want to see.

Equity Uprise is an invite-only fellowship for artists, musicians and organizers who turn what their community needs into work people can hear, share and act on. No open enrollment: fellows are nominated, invited and onboarded together as a cohort.

Request an invitation I have an invite: start onboarding

The fellowship

A small cohort, a shared mission and a public body of work. Each fellow leaves with a finished piece, a public portfolio and a network that keeps working after the showcase.

Invitation

Nominated, not enrolled

Seats are offered by invitation. Anyone can ask to be considered; the cohort is chosen.

Mission

One question, many voices

Every cohort writes to a shared prompt about a change its community needs.

Showcase

Finished in public

The cohort closes with a live capstone and a release on the Equity Uprise record.

From invite to fellow

  1. Request or receive an invite.
    Nominations and requests are reviewed by the cohort team.
  2. Accept and sign in.
    One Equity Uprise account across every house.
  3. Onboard.
    Profile, mission brief and cohort calendar.
  4. Make the work.
    Write, record and present with your cohort.
