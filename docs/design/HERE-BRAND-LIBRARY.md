# HERE Brand Library

## Thesis

HERE is not a streaming-app skin. It is the record's production house.

The work owns the stage. Interface patterns exist to make the work legible, playable, licensable, and memorable. They never substitute generic cards, pills, gradients, or dashboards for an authored scene.

## Non-negotiable rules

1. One dominant job owns the first viewport.
2. A track room is 70 percent shared transport and metadata, 30 percent a song-specific stage.
3. Real photography and real footage are proof. Stock-looking and synthetic-looking imagery are not acceptable substitutes.
4. Audio begins only after visitor intent. Motion must respect reduced-motion preferences.
5. The Music tab remains a persistent transport where a playable deck exists and a door where it does not.
6. The single bottom app bar remains the navigation system. A redesign may not add a second global bar.
7. Ruby marks action, live state, or registered emphasis. It is not general decoration.
8. Rounded glass is reserved for persistent system chrome. Editorial content uses rules, fields, frames, and open space.
9. Every commercial claim is paired with visible work, a measurable fact, or an explicit refusal.
10. HERE, McCluster, Equity Uprise, PRIM3, and Catalog retain their approved spellings.

- Technical identifiers such as QT6KV and ISRC values use tabular numerals, uppercase labels, and explicit spacing.

## Core components

### Living Cover

The release artwork is the opening field, not a thumbnail beside a catalogue shelf.

### Song Stage

Each track translates its premise into composition:

- Who Did The Shoot: production dossier.
- Runway Walk: wide runway field.
- Write a Song: session sheet.
- Here: manifesto.
- Antisocial: midnight scroll.
- Lightroom: edit log.

### Transport Rail

Play, pause, seek, duration, next, and previous use one shared grammar. The rail must never obscure the primary action or important copy.

### Credit Ledger

Writer, performer, producer, album, registration, and licensing information are part of the authored experience, not buried footer copy.

### Registered Catalog

Release artwork and QT6KV registration data live in one information architecture.

### Licensing Brief

A request should feel like a numbered professional brief, disclose the approximate effort, inherit the selected track, and end with a clear desk receipt.

## Motion

- Muted environmental motion may establish a scene.
- Sound always requires visitor intent.
- Reduced motion removes decorative movement without removing content or controls.
- Motion is snappy and purposeful. It cannot delay direct entry, playback, licensing, or navigation.

## Copy grammar

Prefer: the desk, on the record, the room, the house, the stage, the ledger, Have no fear.

Avoid unsupported generic verbs such as discover, unlock, elevate, and revolutionize.
