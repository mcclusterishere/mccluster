# Equity Uprise Development Architecture — Design Rationale

> Status: CANONICAL RATIONALE RECORD v0.1
>
> Purpose: preserve how and why the current development architecture was chosen. Future contributors should not infer intent solely from machine-readable files.

## Why this package exists

The Equity Uprise building began as a representation of existing repo capabilities. As the concept matured, the intended role of the building changed: it is meant to become an operating interface through which cohort members and lifetime participants actually work, learn, produce, prove capability, access tools, and progress toward real opportunities.

That change makes a feature-by-feature building insufficient.

The institution requires a human-development architecture first.

## Decisions preserved here

### 1. Free lifetime development is the base layer

Equity Uprise should offer a free, persistent Lifetime Development relationship.

Participants may later enter specialized paid, funded, selective, or capacity-limited cohorts, but those cohorts sit above the lifetime layer rather than replacing it.

### 2. The Development Passport is the longitudinal state model

The Passport is not a decorative badge page.

It tracks:

- goals and direction;
- learning;
- credentials;
- competency maturity;
- projects and artifacts;
- reviewer evidence;
- relationships;
- opportunities;
- cohort readiness;
- longitudinal history.

### 3. Competence is not course completion

The program intentionally rejects a badge-farm model.

External/federal courses and certificates are evidence of learning. They normally establish Trained status, not Verified mastery.

Higher maturity requires performance, artifacts, independence, authentic context, and qualified review.

### 4. Learn → Apply → Produce → Verify

Development should connect learning to real work.

Where practical:

- learn the concept;
- practice it;
- produce something;
- defend or demonstrate it;
- receive qualified review;
- use it in authentic work.

### 5. Projects are the connective tissue

The institution should not present its tools as disconnected features.

A participant's active project should determine which tools, people, competencies, credentials, and next actions are relevant.

### 6. Progressive disclosure is mandatory

Comprehensive capability must not become cognitive overload.

The system may contain many tools and opportunities, but the participant usually sees:

NEXT → WHY → DO → PROVE → UNLOCKS

Deep exploration remains available but is not the default.

### 7. The Passport tracks maturity, not XP

Canonical maturity states:

Aware
→ Trained
→ Practicing
→ Demonstrated
→ Verified
→ Applied
→ Mentor

These states describe evidence-backed capability, not gamified points.

### 8. AI use is transparent rather than prohibited

AI assistance is expected.

Artifacts record AI-assistance level. Reviewers assess the human competency that the Passport claims.

Where AI materially creates work, evidence defense or live performance may be required to establish human judgment.

### 9. Independence is a first-class assessment property

Canonical assistance levels:

Directed
Supported
Independent
Leading

A high-quality artifact produced with substantive assistance is not automatically evidence of independent performance.

### 10. Authorization and competence are separate

Being highly competent does not grant access to protected systems.

Role/identity authorization, competency prerequisites, project assignment, and explicit approvals may all be required independently.

This is especially important for Halo/Seek First, government submissions, sensitive information, financial operations, and other consequential systems.

### 11. The building follows the journey

The six enclosed floors are expected to align with the six letters in EQUITY.

The Level 7 roof / upward symbol is intended as the culmination.

The actual E-Q-U-I-T-Y acronym words are intentionally deferred until the developmental program and physical program are reconciled.

The current conceptual developmental sequence is:

Enter
→ Participate
→ Connect
→ Build
→ Prove
→ Operate
→ Rise / Launch

These are stage meanings, not locked acronym words.

### 12. The roof represents greater autonomy

The seventh stage should not simply add more coursework.

It represents launch into employment, apprenticeship, fellowships, ventures, leadership, partner opportunities, and mentoring.

Participants can always return to continue developing.

### 13. The physical building is not final

The current Core V2 building is treated as a stable schematic chassis.

Its vertical/core continuity can remain useful while the program authority changes.

Before physical redesign:

- reconcile this development package with all institutional capabilities;
- define actual activity/occupancy/equipment/privacy/acoustic/technology requirements;
- separate physical-program authority from interaction routes;
- decide which existing floor specs are kept, rewritten, or retired;
- only then regenerate plans and 3D.

### 14. Why evidence architecture is strict

The Passport becomes valuable only if outside people can understand what a verified competency means.

Therefore evidence must preserve:

- authenticity;
- relevance;
- quality;
- independence;
- context;
- recency;
- provenance;
- reviewer identity;
- rubric version;
- AI-assistance declaration;
- original artifact version.

### 15. Why one artifact can support multiple competencies

Real work is multidisciplinary.

A single substantial project may produce evidence toward communication, research, project management, media, data, or operations competencies.

Each competency still receives its own rubric assessment.

### 16. Why review is competency-specific

Reviewer authority is not a single global boolean.

A reviewer should only verify competency domains they are authorized and qualified to assess.

### 17. Why failure is treated as development

An assessment result should generally produce:

- what was demonstrated;
- what needs development;
- the next action;
- a path to reassessment.

Critical safety, ethics, privacy, security, consent, rights, or authority failures still block verification where applicable.

## Reconstruction rule

If future architecture appears to conflict with this rationale, contributors should not silently reinterpret the program.

They should document the new decision, its reason, and which previous rule it supersedes.
