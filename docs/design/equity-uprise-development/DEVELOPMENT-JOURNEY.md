# Equity Uprise Lifetime Development Journey

> Status: CANONICAL DEVELOPMENT-JOURNEY AUTHORITY v0.1
>
> This document defines the participant journey independently of current floor names. The future building should support this journey rather than forcing the journey to fit stale room labels.

## 1. Lifetime Development

The baseline Equity Uprise program is free, open-ended, and lifelong.

Joining Equity Uprise creates a persistent Development Passport rather than a temporary enrollment record.

The Passport tracks:

- direction: goals, interests, preferred career/community paths;
- learning: federal and Equity Uprise training completed;
- credentials: certificates/certifications, verification, renewal or expiration;
- competencies: maturity states supported by evidence;
- work: projects, publications, media, research, service, and deliverables;
- proof: artifacts, citations, reviewer assessments, and provenance;
- relationships: mentors, collaborators, organizations, and cohort participation;
- opportunities: fellowships, apprenticeships, jobs, projects, and enterprise paths;
- cohort readiness: which advanced programs the participant is ready to pursue;
- longitudinal history: development over time rather than a single snapshot.

The Passport is not merely a resume. It is the state model that drives recommendations, progression, and evidence.

## 2. Default participant experience

The interface should ask a simple initial question:

What are you trying to become or accomplish?

The participant provides goals, interests, experience, and a small baseline assessment.

The system then recommends one primary next action.

Canonical interaction grammar:

NEXT → WHY → DO → PROVE → UNLOCKS

The system may internally know about hundreds of resources, but it should not display all of them at once.

## 3. Seven developmental stages

### Stage 1 — Enter

Human transformation:

I know where I am, what I want, and how to function inside this institution.

Typical activity:

- orientation;
- goals and baseline profile;
- Passport creation;
- account/tool setup;
- foundational professional/safety literacy;
- initial training.

Primary competency homes:

- CORE-01 Self-Direction
- CORE-05 Digital Fluency
- CORE-06 Civic & Institutional Literacy
- CORE-07 Professional Practice
- CORE-08 Safety & Resilience

Expected maturity:

Mostly Aware → Trained.

### Stage 2 — Participate

Human transformation:

I can communicate, listen, and work constructively with other people.

Typical activity:

- discussion;
- active listening;
- presentation;
- meetings;
- collaboration;
- accessibility/inclusion practice;
- community participation.

Primary competency homes:

- CORE-02 Communication
- CORE-03 Collaboration
- SHR-05 Facilitation & Meetings
- SHR-06 Accessible & Inclusive Practice
- PSC-01 Community Engagement

Expected maturity:

Mostly Trained → Practicing.

### Stage 3 — Connect

Human transformation:

I have people, opportunities, service pathways, and real-world relationships around me.

Typical activity:

- mentors and fellows;
- organizations;
- service navigation;
- stakeholder relationships;
- interviews;
- VITA or similar service;
- fellowship/apprenticeship discovery;
- customer/user discovery;
- career navigation.

Primary competency homes:

- SHR-02 Ethics, Consent & Privacy
- PSC-02 Service Navigation
- PSC-03 Stakeholder Coordination
- PSC-04 Public Program Delivery
- ENT-01 Opportunity Discovery
- ENT-02 User / Customer Discovery
- ENT-06 Workforce & Career Navigation

Expected maturity:

Real supervised participation begins.

### Stage 4 — Build

Human transformation:

I can make something useful for somebody.

Typical activity:

- bounded project ownership;
- scoping;
- media production;
- technical workflows;
- client/project delivery;
- automation;
- concrete deliverables.

Primary competency homes:

- SHR-01 Project Management
- ENT-03 Offer & Scope Design
- ENT-05 Client / Project Delivery
- MED-01 Story & Message Development
- MED-02 Capture & Production
- MED-03 Editing & Post-Production
- OPS-06 Automation & Tooling

Expected maturity:

Learning becomes observable production.

### Stage 5 — Prove

Human transformation:

I can establish that my work is accurate, ethical, attributable, and defensible.

Typical activity:

- research;
- source evaluation;
- data analysis;
- human-research methods;
- evidence synthesis;
- policy analysis;
- publication;
- citations;
- provenance;
- rights/licensing;
- audience/impact analysis.

Primary competency homes:

- CORE-04 Research Literacy
- SHR-03 Data Literacy
- SHR-04 Documentation & Provenance
- RSP-01 Research Design
- RSP-02 Source Evaluation
- RSP-03 Human Research Methods
- RSP-04 Evidence Synthesis
- RSP-05 Policy Analysis
- RSP-06 Publication & Citation
- MED-04 Verification, Rights & Licensing
- MED-05 Publication, Distribution & Audience

Expected maturity:

Demonstrated → Verified.

### Stage 6 — Operate

Human transformation:

I can function responsibly inside consequential systems.

Typical activity:

- emergency/incident operations;
- financial/operational planning;
- systems thinking;
- cybersecurity;
- spatial intelligence;
- infrastructure/energy analysis;
- operational decision support;
- advanced tool use.

Primary competency homes:

- PSC-05 Emergency & Incident Coordination
- ENT-04 Financial & Operational Basics
- OPS-01 Systems Thinking
- OPS-02 Cybersecurity Fundamentals
- OPS-03 Spatial Intelligence
- OPS-04 Infrastructure & Energy Literacy
- OPS-05 Operational Decision Support

Expected maturity:

Verified → Applied.

### Stage 7 — Rise / Launch

Human transformation:

I can take what I have built into the world with substantially greater autonomy.

No new competency IDs live here.

This stage evaluates and combines competencies developed below it.

Typical outcomes:

- employment;
- Registered Apprenticeship;
- advanced fellowship;
- specialized Equity Uprise cohort;
- venture/enterprise path;
- independent project leadership;
- partner opportunity;
- mentoring;
- alumni/lifetime development.

Expected maturity:

Applied → Mentor / independent practitioner.

Stage 7 is not a permanent exit from Equity Uprise. Participants may return for new projects, credentials, pathways, mentoring, or advanced development.

## 4. Competencies continue vertically

A competency has a primary home stage but can mature across later stages.

Example: Communication.

- Stage 2: listening and presenting;
- Stage 3: stakeholder interview;
- Stage 4: project/media communication;
- Stage 5: accurate evidence communication;
- Stage 6: decision-maker briefing;
- Stage 7: mentoring another participant.

The Passport tracks maturity, not merely floor completion.

## 5. Projects as connective tissue

Every participant should eventually have an active project.

Projects create meaningful reasons to use the institution's tools and allow one authentic artifact to support several competencies.

Progressive project authority:

assist
→ contribute
→ own a deliverable
→ own a project
→ lead a team
→ mentor

The system should recommend tools in the context of the participant's project instead of presenting a giant standalone tool catalog.

## 6. Credentials

External or federal credentials support competency evidence.

They do not automatically equal mastery.

Typical relationship:

training certificate → Trained

then:

exercise / artifact → Practicing or Demonstrated

then:

qualified review → Verified

then:

authentic real-world use → Applied

Examples may include FEMA, IRS VITA, EPA, HHS/OHRP, NIH, ATSDR/CDC, CISA, DOE, NOAA, NASA, or other validated programs. Each external program must be recorded accurately according to what the issuer actually awards.

## 7. Cohorts

Free Lifetime Development is the persistent base layer.

Specialized cohorts may exist above it, including research, media, infrastructure, public service, enterprise, or other programs.

The Passport should show readiness transparently:

- requirements satisfied;
- requirements remaining;
- recommended next action.

Readiness or eligibility does not guarantee admission where capacity or other legitimate requirements apply.

## 8. Autonomy progression

The institution should gradually shift from directing the participant toward enabling self-direction.

Early:

Do this next.

Middle:

Choose among these appropriate projects.

Later:

Propose your own project and request resources.

Advanced:

Lead a team.

Then:

Mentor someone behind you.

The final developmental objective is not permanent dependence on the institution. It is increased verified capability and autonomy.
