## Rule

A federal agency name is not a course.

Equity Uprise may present a federal training as active only when the catalog has:
- an exact provider;
- exact title and course code where one exists;
- official federal URL;
- current status verification;
- accurate award semantics;
- prerequisites;
- revalidation rule when the training is annual/versioned.

Federal/external training remains **training evidence**, not automatic competency mastery.

## Annual programs

VITA/TCE is tax-year/version sensitive. The catalog's active cycle must be reverified before each new learner cohort. A prior-year record may remain as historical evidence but must not silently become the current course.

## Safety and authorization

Course completion never grants:
- live B1/tunnel access;
- live electrical/HVAC/BAS/security/fire-alarm control;
- real taxpayer access outside an authorized VITA/TCE site;
- IRB authority;
- emergency-response authority;
- code/AHJ approval.

Simulation first. Authentic work only through the appropriate real program and authorization.
