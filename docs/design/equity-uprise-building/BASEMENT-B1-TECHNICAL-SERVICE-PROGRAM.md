# Equity Uprise — B1 Technical / Service Basement

> Status: **CANONICAL SUPPORT-LEVEL PROGRAM v0.1**
>
> B1 is **not** part of the E-Q-U-I-T-Y developmental floor sequence.

## Purpose

B1 gives the virtual headquarters a believable technical backbone and a persistent building-systems training environment.

It houses conceptual mechanical, electrical/emergency power, fire-protection/water, network/telecom, flood-management, facilities/service, and building-systems lab functions.

Normal public users do not browse B1 as another floor of content. Authorized learners, instructors and staff may access it through Building Systems Lab or simulation modes.

## Level

- Display level: **B1**
- Finished-floor elevation: **-13'-6"**
- Floor 1 remains the level of exit discharge at 0'-0".
- B1 uses the same 72' × 72' coordination footprint.
- Stair A, Stair B, passenger elevator, service/freight elevator and MEP riser continue to B1.

Shared vertical-core geometry remains authoritative.

## Why B1 exists

B1 makes it possible to run meaningful simulations involving:

- building power loss and emergency transfer;
- mechanical failure;
- building automation;
- network/core failures;
- water intrusion/flooding;
- fire-protection system state;
- service/maintenance incidents;
- accessibility and evacuation from below grade;
- critical-operations shutdown;
- infrastructure interdependency analysis.

## Egress / accessibility concept

Protected Stair A and Stair B continue from B1 to Floor 1.

Passenger elevator serves B1 as an accessible/operational route in the model, but emergency use depends on the selected simulation profile and scenario authority.

Floor 1 includes barriers/wayfinding that prevent occupants from unintentionally following an exit stair down into B1 during evacuation.
