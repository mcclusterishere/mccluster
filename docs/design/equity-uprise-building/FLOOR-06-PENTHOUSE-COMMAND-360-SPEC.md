## 1. Repo-derived purpose

Floor 6 spatializes the highest-level institutional view already present across Equity Uprise.

### `equity-uprise.html` — Institutional Desk

The public-facing institutional desk is already organized around three high-level states:

1. **NOW**
   - current public-affairs/policy-intelligence work;
   - live infrastructure/data-center governance work;
   - current research and working-paper architecture;
   - current institutional activity.

2. **PAST WORK**
   - Docket 516/516R;
   - prior campaign/media work;
   - October 5 convening;
   - policy memo programs;
   - infrastructure work;
   - credential/proof records.

3. **JOIN**
   - fellows;
   - organizations;
   - operators/developers/utilities/builders/suppliers;
   - residents/environmental/ratepayer groups;
   - foundations/universities/neutral institutions;
   - live-issue intake.

Those three states define the public-facing identity of Floor 6.

### `eu-status`, `eu-control`, monitoring and publication systems

The repo also contains internal systems for:
- integration health;
- jobs;
- monitors;
- approvals;
- commands;
- publication state;
- fellowship/interview queues;
- control-plane actions.

These justify a secure back-of-house systems/support layer.

### Boundary with other floors

- Floor 2 owns public conversation.
- Floor 3 owns fellows/network/opportunities.
- Floor 4 owns media/culture.
- Floor 5 owns research/evidence/publication review.
- Floor 6 owns the **institutional overview and directional layer**.

Floor 6 summarizes and routes; it does not duplicate the detailed workspaces below.
