# Equity Uprise — Exterior Facade Upgrade Spec

> Status: **ACTIVE FACADE DESIGN AUTHORITY — GEOMETRY IMPLEMENTATION PENDING**
>
> Companion authority: `BUILDING-SYSTEMS-AND-FACADE-CORE-RULES.md`.
>
> **Not for construction.**

## Design intent

Convert the existing stacked digital twin into a believable modern civic / research / media institution with a coherent exterior enclosure.

The facade should feel serious, contemporary and durable — not a generic office box, glass toy, sci-fi fortress or nightclub.

## Composition

### Base — Floor 1 / lower public face
- strongest pedestrian scale;
- principal public entrance hierarchy;
- greater transparency where public-facing program supports it;
- more materially grounded base;
- entry canopy / recess / vestibule expression;
- visible Equity Uprise identity.

### Middle — Floors 2–5
- disciplined repeated facade rhythm;
- substantial glazing on all major sides where compatible with rooms behind;
- consistent mullion / frame / spandrel logic;
- opaque/service zones aligned with actual core and support conditions;
- no blank walls without functional or compositional reason.

### Crown — Floor 6 / Level 7
- slightly more refined upper-level expression;
- penthouse glazing coordinated with Floor 6;
- roof parapet / equipment screen / stair-headhouse composition;
- restrained crown identity;
- no fake aviation architecture.

## Glazing rules

- provide a coherent window or curtain-wall system on **all major elevations**;
- glazing must correspond to actual interior conditions;
- public / collaborative / office-like rooms may receive larger glazing fields;
- technical/core/service walls may remain opaque;
- B1 remains below-grade and windowless;
- stair glazing, if used, remains subordinate to protected-stair logic;
- no exterior door, balcony or terrace may be invented on Floors 2–6 unless separately authorized.

## Facade depth

Later geometry must include more than flat colored planes:
- mullion depth;
- sill/head/jamb depth;
- slab-edge or spandrel expression;
- panel joints / reveals;
- corner conditions;
- entrance framing;
- parapet caps;
- roof/service screens;
- believable transitions between glass and opaque systems.

## Material family

Current design direction:
- clear / neutral glazing;
- charcoal / blackened / dark-bronze metal framing;
- warm gray mineral / precast / stone-like base surfaces;
- dark architectural metal panels in service/crown zones;
- restrained warm material at the principal entrance;
- limited Equity Uprise accent color.

Final texture maps, manufacturers and construction assemblies are not yet selected.

## Functional coordination

The facade may not:
- cover stair or elevator access requirements;
- conceal required service intake/exhaust without a modeled strategy;
- place glass through opaque service/equipment zones without reason;
- conflict with interior walls/ceilings;
- ignore roof drainage, parapets or equipment screening.

## Exterior realism checklist for later implementation

- all four elevations intentionally composed;
- main entrance visually obvious;
- glazing rhythm aligns vertically;
- corner conditions resolved;
- opaque service zones align with real building functions;
- base / middle / crown legible;
- roof equipment visually screened but serviceable;
- E = U / Equity Uprise identity integrated;
- day and night facade states both read plausibly.
