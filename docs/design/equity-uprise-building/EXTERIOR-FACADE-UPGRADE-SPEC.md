## 1. Design intent

Convert the current 72' × 72' stacked digital twin from a visually exposed floor-stack into one coherent **civic / research / media institution** with a credible exterior envelope.

The exterior should read as:
- contemporary;
- serious;
- transparent where people/public program benefit from daylight and visibility;
- opaque where acoustic, service, privacy, core or equipment conditions require it;
- architecturally branded as Equity Uprise;
- materially substantial rather than cartoonishly glossy;
- consistent from the street through the crown.

It must not read as:
- a generic glass box;
- an unbroken concrete cube;
- a sci-fi fortress;
- a nightclub;
- a stack of unrelated floor scenes;
- a facade pasted over interior geometry without coordination.

## 2. Chassis preservation

The existing Core V2 geometry remains controlling:

- primary exterior control plane: **72' × 72'**;
- southwest datum: **(0,0)**;
- floor-to-floor: **13.5 ft**;
- B1 FFE: **-13.5 ft**;
- F1: **0 ft**;
- F2: **13.5 ft**;
- F3: **27 ft**;

The facade may replace simplified perimeter-wall presentation, add depth, mullions, fins, canopy, screens, trim and branding, but it may not casually move:
- floor elevations;
- protected stairs;
- passenger elevator;
- freight/service elevator;
- slab openings;
- MEP/riser reservation;
- approved room/program geometry.

### Exterior control-plane rule

The primary weather/enclosure plane remains coordinated to the existing exterior control planes:
- west: **X=0**;
- east: **X=72**;
- south: **Y=0**;
- north: **Y=72**.

Mullions, caps, fins and trim may project modestly beyond that plane for rendering/architectural depth. The entrance canopy is an intentional exception and may project south of Y=0.

## 3. Facade module / mathematical rhythm

The facade must use the existing 18 ft structural coordination grid instead of an unrelated decorative rhythm.

### Horizontal module

Each 72 ft elevation is divided into:
- **4 primary structural bays @ 18 ft**;
- each 18 ft bay subdivided into **3 secondary facade modules @ 6 ft**;
- total: **12 secondary modules per elevation**.

Canonical secondary module lines:
**0, 6, 12, 18, 24, 30, 36, 42, 48, 54, 60, 66, 72 ft**.

Primary vertical emphasis aligns with:
**0, 18, 36, 54, 72 ft**.

This gives the building a repeated mathematical language while still allowing localized opaque, fritted or service modules.

### Conceptual frame geometry

For deterministic visualization:
- typical mullion face width: approximately **0.25 ft**;
- typical mullion/frame depth: approximately **0.35 ft**;
- primary 18 ft-grid fin/pier may read approximately **0.6–0.9 ft wide** and project approximately **0.4–0.7 ft** where used;
- panel/reveal joints should remain visually subordinate to the 6 ft module.

These are rendering coordination values, not fabricated curtain-wall dimensions.

## 4. Vertical facade rhythm

Every 13.5 ft occupied story should read as a complete facade story rather than one undifferentiated wall.

### Floors 2–5 typical coordination band

Starting coordination target per 13.5 ft story:
- lower opaque / sill / slab-edge zone: approximately **0–1.5 ft AFF**;
- primary vision zone: approximately **1.5–10.5 ft AFF**;
- upper spandrel / ceiling / service zone: approximately **10.5–13.5 ft AFF**.

This is a visual coordination datum. Interior ceiling/system design may refine exact transitions later.

### Floor 1 base

Floor 1 may use taller transparent fields and a stronger mineral/metal base:
- public vision/entry field may extend approximately **0.5–11.0 ft AFF**;
- upper entry/spandrel/signage band approximately **11.0–13.5 ft AFF**;
- solid plinth conditions may rise higher in selected side/support zones.

### Floor 6 crown

Floor 6 should be visibly related to the middle floors but slightly more refined:
- larger / cleaner vision fields in public-facing areas;
- stronger upper crown band approximately **10.25–13.5 ft AFF**;
- more deliberate vertical fin/pier emphasis at the 18 ft grid;
- north/core zones remain substantially opaque.

## 5. Base / middle / crown composition

### Base — Floor 1

The base must clearly communicate **arrival**.

Required expression:
- principal public entrance on south elevation;
- glazed vestibule visible behind the entrance;
- grounded mineral/stone-like base material;
- stronger entrance frame;
- weather canopy;
- E = U / Equity Uprise identity integrated with the entry composition;
- transparent public-facing bays balanced by opaque structural/service areas.

### Middle — Floors 2–5

The middle floors should read as one institutional body:
- repeated 6 ft facade module;
- consistent mullion family;
- consistent slab-edge/spandrel family;
- controlled floor-specific privacy/acoustic changes without destroying the overall rhythm;
- no random window sizes by floor.

### Crown — Floor 6 + Level 7 edge

The crown should:
- visually terminate the six occupied floors;
- strengthen Floor 6 without becoming a luxury penthouse trope;
- integrate the roof parapet, stair headhouses and service screens;
- conceal rooftop clutter from normal street views where feasible;
- preserve the Level 7 program and protected stair arrivals;
- carry one optional secondary E = U architectural mark.

## 6. South elevation — primary civic face

The south elevation is the principal public identity elevation.

### Floor 1 entrance lock

The existing vestibule is approximately **X29–43 / Y0–9**.

Coordinate the facade as:
- entrance composition zone: approximately **X24–48**;
- primary paired-door / main opening zone: approximately **X30–42**;
- side glazing / sidelights fill the remainder of the entrance composition;
- canopy: approximately **X24–48**, projecting up to about **5.5 ft south of Y=0**, subject to future site/egress coordination;
- entrance frame should align with the 6 ft facade rhythm;
- the south entrance remains the dominant public entry.

No later facade pass may move the entrance away from the existing vestibule without a formal Floor 1/Core coordination revision.

### Floors 2–6

South-facing upper floors remain **sealed glazing/windows**. No balcony, terrace or exterior door is added.

Target character:
- generally high transparency;
- approximate visual glazing target: **75–90% of the available vision field**, excluding localized acoustic/privacy zones;
- clear glazing for open/collaborative areas;
- fritted / tinted / internally shaded glazing where meeting/privacy needs require it;
- opaque acoustic facade where recording-room performance genuinely requires it.

### Floor-specific south modulation

- **F2 Public Forum:** predominantly clear sealed glazing.
- **F3 Fellowship + Network:** south meeting/interview rooms may use privacy frit/gradient while retaining daylight.
- **F4 Media + Culture:** Creator Recording Room zone may become substantially opaque/acoustic; Edit/Review may use controlled/fritted glazing; remaining bays stay transparent.
- **F5 Policy + Proof:** review rooms may use privacy/frit treatment rather than eliminating the facade rhythm.
- **F6 Penthouse Command:** Strategy Review / Partner Briefing may use high-performance privacy treatment; open command/Halo-side south-east zones remain visually lighter.

## 7. East elevation

The east elevation should present a lighter public/occupied face across the south/middle portion and a more solid protected-core expression toward the north.

### Y0–54 occupied/public band
- repeated 6 ft glazing rhythm;
- target visual glazing in vision field: approximately **60–80%**;
- floor-specific opaque modules only where interior acoustics/privacy/support require them.

### Y54–72 Stair A / north service band
- substantially more opaque;
- optional narrow/vertical translucent or fire-rated-looking stair-light slots may be represented conceptually without claiming final rating/compliance;
- MEP/service zone should use opaque architectural panels and/or coordinated louver fields;
- no random clear curtain wall through the stair/MEP zone.

This creates a legible vertical service/core spine on the east-north corner.

## 8. West elevation

### Y0–54 occupied/public band
- repeated 6 ft facade rhythm;
- target visual glazing in vision field: approximately **55–75%**;
- meeting/media/acoustic zones may use selective opacity/frit.

### Y54–72 west service core
The west service/freight/core zone must read as intentionally more solid:
- opaque metal/mineral panel family;
- limited service louvers where later MEP coordination requires them;
- optional controlled stair daylight where compatible;
- no assumption of an exterior loading dock or freight door until site/loading authority explicitly creates one.

The facade must not invent an exterior service opening merely because the freight elevator exists internally.

## 9. North elevation — service/support face

The north elevation is not required to mimic the south civic face.

It contains substantial support/core adjacency:
- freight/service shaft area;
- Stair B;
- restrooms/support band;
- MEP/service reservation;
- Stair A.

Therefore the north facade should be the most opaque elevation.

Target:
- approximately **15–35% visual glazing/opening** overall;
- narrow controlled stair-light glazing;
- high clerestory/support glazing only where useful;
- architectural louvers aligned with genuine MEP/service needs;
- strong opaque panel rhythm on the same 6 ft module;
- no fake office curtain wall in front of service/core conditions.

The north facade should still look designed, not like a forgotten blank backside.

## 10. Corner conditions

The four corners must be intentionally resolved.

Rules:
- mullion/panel geometry wraps cleanly around corners;
- glass-to-glass corners are allowed only where interior conditions support them;
- otherwise use a dark metal/mineral corner pier;
- primary 18 ft rhythm should visually turn the corner rather than terminate randomly;
- slab-edge bands align continuously around the building.

Suggested character:
- south-east and south-west corners may read lighter/more glazed;
- north-east and north-west corners may read heavier because of core/service adjacency.

## 11. Floor 4 acoustic exception

Floor 4 is the main exception to a simple all-glass aesthetic.

The Creator Recording Room must not be forced into full-height clear glazing just to satisfy a facade percentage.

Its south/west-adjacent facade may use:
- opaque acoustic panel/spandrel;
- smaller controlled window;
- high clerestory;
- double-layer visual treatment in later detailed design.

The facade rhythm must remain visible through frame/panel joints even where glass becomes opaque.

## 12. Entrance architecture

The entrance must feel like an actual institutional front door.

Required later geometry:
- recessed or framed opening;
- paired public entry doors;
- sidelights / vestibule glazing;
- canopy with real thickness;
- canopy supports or credible cantilever expression;
- threshold / base material transition;
- architectural lighting;
- E = U identity;
- Equity Uprise wordmark;
- building directory/wayfinding adjacency.

The entry should not be represented as a hole cut in a flat wall.

## 13. Roof edge / crown / service screening

Level 7 remains open-air.

Exterior integration must preserve:
- existing parapet/guard geometry;
- Stair A and Stair B headhouses;
- passenger-elevator overrun/service enclosure;
- freight/service reservation;
- north MEP/service screens;
- roof circulation and mobility-reservation logic.

### Crown treatment

Use a coordinated family of:
- dark metal parapet caps;
- vertical/linear screen elements;
- roof-service screens approximately consistent with current 6 ft-high service-screen authority;
- refined Floor 6 upper band.

Rooftop screens must hide clutter without creating a fake solid seventh enclosed floor.

## 14. Exterior material system

The facade should use a restrained, physically legible family.

### FAC-GLASS-01 — primary vision glass
Visual direction:
- neutral / low-iron appearance;
- slight cool-neutral reflection;
- transparent enough to read inhabited interiors;
- not mirror-black;
- not bright blue corporate glass.

### FAC-GLASS-02 — privacy/frit glass
Use in:
- interview/briefing/review rooms;
- selective service/privacy transitions.

It should remain part of the same glass family, not look like a different building.

### FAC-METAL-01 — mullions / frames / fins
- charcoal / blackened bronze / dark gunmetal;
- low-to-medium gloss;
- enough roughness to catch light without chrome appearance.

### FAC-OPAQUE-01 — spandrel / architectural metal panel
- dark charcoal / bronze-black;
- used at slab edges, service/core zones and crown.

### FAC-MINERAL-01 — grounded base
- warm gray stone / precast / honed mineral character;
- visually heavier than upper facade;
- concentrated primarily on Floor 1/base.

### FAC-WARM-01 — entrance accent
- restrained warm bronze / wood-like / warm-metal material;
- entrance only or very limited institutional thresholds;
- never spread across the entire tower.

Final products, fire performance, thermal performance and fabrication assemblies remain unresolved.

## 15. Branding integration

Primary branding is governed by `FACADE-BRANDING-AND-SIGNAGE-SPEC.md`.

For facade coordination, lock:

### Primary entry identity
- south elevation;
- centered around building/entry axis near **X36**;
- integrated with the entrance frame/canopy/spandrel;
- E = U principal mark;
- Equity Uprise institutional wordmark.

### Secondary crown mark
One restrained secondary **E = U** may be integrated into the south crown/Floor 6 upper band, centered approximately on the primary building axis.

It must:
- fit within the facade module;
- have a real backing/mounting plane;
- not cover vision glass arbitrarily;
- remain smaller than a billboard;
- not create a second competing wordmark system.

## 16. Exterior lighting

Exterior lighting is architectural support, not decoration.

Later implementation should include:
- warm entry/canopy lighting;
- restrained sign backlighting/halo lighting;
- limited vertical grazing at selected base/primary fins;
- interior night glow visible through glazing;
- low-level roof/crown lighting already coordinated with Level 7;
- no continuous neon outline around the whole building;
- no excessive bloom.

Night mode should make the building look occupied and institutional, not entertainment-driven.

## 17. Service / ventilation openings

Louvers and vents may appear only where supported by later MEP coordination.

Rules:
- align louver fields to the 6 ft module;
- place them primarily on north/east/west service/core portions;
- avoid the principal south civic face unless functionally required;
- do not add decorative louvers with no system behind them;
- do not expose uncoordinated ducts through the facade.

## 18. Drainage / weather expression

The digital twin should acknowledge that the facade/roof handles weather.

Later geometry should coordinate:
- parapet caps;
- roof drainage to the storm system;
- entry canopy drainage concept;
- sill/head/drip expression where visible;
- base transition above grade.

No fake exposed downspouts should be added unless the storm-routing authority actually selects them.

## 19. Interior/facade coordination

Before any facade module is built, the implementation pass must check what is directly behind it.

Every 6 ft facade module must be classified as one of:
- clear vision;
- privacy/frit vision;
- acoustic/opaque;
- service/opaque;
- louver/service;
- stair/core glazing;
- entrance/door;
- crown/parapet.

A facade module may not be selected independently of:
- room use;
- ceiling height;
- interior wall location;
- display placement;
- acoustic/privacy requirement;
- MEP/service need.
