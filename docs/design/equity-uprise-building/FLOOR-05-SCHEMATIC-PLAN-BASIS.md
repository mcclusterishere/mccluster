## 15. Archive/load logic

Paper/file storage can create concentrated loads.

At schematic level:
- keep heavier secure storage primarily in the north support band and west archive zone;
- avoid pretending large compact shelving is weightless;
- final archive loads must be coordinated with structural engineering.

---

This schematic preserves:

- fixed elevator/core;
- stacked services;
- open circulation.

- occupancy classification;
- occupant load;
- exit separation;
- travel distance;
- stair sizing;
- rated assemblies;
- sprinkler/fire-alarm design;
- archive fire protection;
- plumbing fixture count;
- structural design;
- MEP design;
- zoning.

Licensed professional review is required.
