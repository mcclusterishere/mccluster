# Equity Uprise — Building Services / Nervous System Implementation Plan

> Status: **ACTIVE IMPLEMENTATION SEQUENCE**
>
> **Not for construction.** This is digital-twin coordination, not engineered MEP documentation.

## Objective

Turn the building from a furnished architectural model into a serviced digital twin.

Every system must trace:

**source / plant → vertical riser → floor branch → representative endpoint → monitoring / service access**

## Services Step 1 — backbone coordination

- fixed service reservation remains **X50–60 / Y66–72**;
- nine conceptual system risers are now allocated inside it;
- the X59–60 east band remains a separation/access buffer against Stair A;
- B1 through L7 receive services addenda before branch geometry is drawn;
- no canonical room, stair, elevator, door or primary circulation may move to fit services.

## Services Step 2 — real vertical backbone geometry

Generate a dedicated services GLB for normal power, emergency power, data, controls, HVAC, domestic water, sanitary/vent, storm and fire protection. Add a viewer layer toggle so the nervous system can actually be exposed/hidden.

## Services Step 3 — B1 plant connections

Connect the modeled mechanical plant, electrical/emergency room, telecom core, fire/water room, sump/flood management and BAS observation to the vertical backbone.

## Services Step 4 — Floors 1–3 branches

Route representative branches/endpoints for arrival/security, forum AV/table power-data, fellowship/interview/network, restrooms, HVAC and life safety.

## Services Step 5 — Floors 4–6 branches

Add higher-density distribution for media/recording/editing, policy/archive/review and command/Halo/briefing systems.

## Services Step 6 — Level 7 terminations

Coordinate roof mechanical equipment, roof drains, path lighting, emergency communications, fire support, lightning-protection concept and service-screen routes.

## Services Step 7 — interior device / ceiling realism

Coordinate panels, racks, trays, conduits, ducts/diffusers/grilles, valves/pipes, access panels, detectors/strobes, extinguishers, readers/intercoms/cameras, receptacles/floor boxes and authorized plumbing/service fixtures.

## Services Step 8 — whole-building verification

Require vertical continuity B1→L7, no core/circulation clashes, traceability from source to endpoints, working floor isolation and viewer layers for Architecture vs Services.

## Current stop point

Services Step 1 is coordination only. **Next is real 3D vertical-backbone geometry and a Services viewer layer.**
