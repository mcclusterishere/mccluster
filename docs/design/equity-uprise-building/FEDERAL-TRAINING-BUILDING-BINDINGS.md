## Purpose

This authority converts federal learning into practical Equity Uprise work without pretending a completion certificate equals mastery.

The canonical progression is:

**federal learning → Equity Uprise simulation/practice → evidence artifact/performance → qualified review → competency maturity**

## Usable now

Current building state can already support facilitated practical work for:
- FEMA ICS / exercise / emergency-planning courses;
- IRS VITA/TCE through the IRS Practice Lab plus a synthetic Floor 1 intake clinic;
- HHS/OHRP through synthetic protocol, privacy, and informed-consent cases.

The Step 4A electronics fabric also provides a strong substrate for future CISA ICS/OT training, but CISA remains proposed until a current exact course/version/access path is approved.

## What still needs runtime work

For fully repeatable labs, add:
- scenario state injection;
- deterministic reset/replay;
- event timeline;
- instructor/evaluator roles;
- evidence capture;
- scoring/rubric integration;
- synthetic case/document packs;
- course prerequisite gating in the Development Passport.

## Safety boundary

No federal course or Equity Uprise competency unlocks live operational systems. B1/tunnel training stays sandboxed. Life-safety, electrical, fire-alarm, BAS/OT, physical-security and other consequential systems remain simulation-only until separately engineered, commissioned and authorized.
