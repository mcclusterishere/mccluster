# Equity Uprise — Underground Tunnel Network Authority

> Status: **CANONICAL UNDERGROUND-BACKBONE AUTHORITY v0.1**
>
> Scope: B1 tunnel interface + future inter-building underground network.
>
> The tunnel network is **not** an E-Q-U-I-T-Y developmental floor and is **not** a public navigation destination.

## 1. Purpose

The underground network is the restricted physical/digital backbone connecting Equity Uprise headquarters to future ecosystem buildings.

Architectural metaphor:

- **↑ Roof** = outward/public ecosystem gateway and launch layer.
- **E-Q-U-I-T-Y floors** = participant development journey.
- **B1** = restricted infrastructure and underground-operations hub.
- **↓ Tunnel network** = private inter-building infrastructure, logistics and transportation backbone.

The network is modeled as industrial campus infrastructure, not as a decorative secret passage.

## 2. Access authority

Live B1/tunnel access is restricted to:

- McCluster house owner; and
- a dedicated `underground_operations_admin` authority explicitly delegated by the house owner.

Ordinary Equity Uprise roles — including member, host, editor, client, staff and ordinary Equity Uprise admin — do **not** gain live B1/tunnel access.

The live B1 level and tunnel network are omitted from normal:
- elevator selectors;
- building directories;
- Journey Wall navigation;
- participant wayfinding;
- public presence maps.

### Training access

Learners/instructors may use a **sandboxed training clone** of B1/tunnel geometry.

Training access:
- does not unlock the live B1;
- does not reveal live tunnel occupancy, infrastructure state, route status or connected-building operational data;
- cannot operate live doors, vehicles, utilities, controls or security systems;
- may simulate incidents and generate competency evidence.

Competency never substitutes for operational authorization.

## 3. B1 physical interface

B1 contains two new canonical underground-network spaces:

### Tunnel Operations Concourse / Security Gate
- approximate planning zone: **X20–48 / Y54–64**
- secure personnel processing;
- live tunnel occupancy/accountability;
- transport dispatch;
- incident/status board;
- inter-building route authorization;
- transition between B1 technical plant and the tunnel system.

### Tunnel Portal / Transfer Lock
- approximate planning zone: **X24–44 / Y64–72**
- controlled transition through the north B1 perimeter;
- access-control interlock;
- environmental/fire separation concept;
- vehicle/personnel transfer boundary;
- emergency closure state.

The tunnel itself extends beyond the 72' × 72' building shell and belongs to a future campus/ecosystem masterplan.

## 4. Tunnel backbone

The tunnel is an industrial multimodal service corridor.

Canonical subsystems:

### Personnel route
A protected walking/inspection path separated from moving equipment where practical.

### Automated electric transport
A low-speed autonomous electric shuttle/utility-vehicle system for authorized personnel and logistics.

The network is not modeled as a high-speed or speculative transport system.

### Freight / logistics
Controlled movement of equipment, parts, media/technology assets and building supplies between connected facilities.

### Utility spine
Reserved distribution concepts for:
- electrical power;
- fiber/data;
- controls/telemetry;
- emergency communications;
- water/thermal-service concepts where future buildings require them.

### Ventilation
Normal and emergency ventilation states, including sector isolation and smoke-control simulation hooks.

### Drainage / flood management
Trench/drain/sump concepts, water sensors, pumps, sector closures and flood-routing scenarios.

### Fire / life safety
Detection, notification, suppression concepts, emergency communication, compartment/closure states and evacuation routing.

### Cyber-physical controls
Vehicle dispatch, doors, sensors, ventilation, pumps, lighting, access control and network segmentation exist as stateful simulated systems.

## 5. Network topology

The network begins at Equity Uprise B1 and reaches a **Central Underground Exchange** outside the current building shell.

Future branch connections are created only when a destination building has its own canonical authority.

Until then, branch interfaces are represented only as:

**FUTURE CONNECTION RESERVED**

Do not invent destination buildings merely to populate the tunnel map.

Each future connected building must define:
- destination ID;
- tunnel interface geometry;
- security boundary;
- utility services received/sent;
- emergency isolation behavior;
- transportation stop;
- route/occupancy semantics.

## 6. Operational model

The live control plane tracks at minimum:

- authorized people underground;
- vehicle/shuttle occupancy;
- current vehicle positions;
- open/closed tunnel sectors;
- destination/branch availability;
- access-control state;
- ventilation state;
- fire/life-safety state;
- drainage/flood state;
- electrical/communications state;
- emergency closures;
- incident command/status;
- maintenance restrictions.

The system must be able to answer:

**Who is underground, where are they, and what route can they safely use?**

## 7. Training / simulation value

The underground environment can support scenarios such as:

- unaccounted-for person underground;
- shuttle/vehicle disabled between buildings;
- tunnel sector fire/smoke event;
- ventilation failure;
- flood/water intrusion;
- electrical feeder loss;
- network/control-system outage;
- cyber compromise of a tunnel subsystem;
- access-control failure;
- hazardous/service spill;
- connected-building isolation;
- multi-building evacuation/re-routing;
- critical utility interruption.

Potential competency evidence includes:

- CORE-05 Digital Fluency
- CORE-07 Professional Practice

- PSC-05 Emergency & Incident Coordination
- OPS-01 Systems Thinking

## 8. Training references

The simulation may draw from real-world concepts found in:
- OSHA underground-construction requirements and underground accountability;
- OSHA permit-required confined-space concepts where a modeled space actually meets the scenario definition;
- FEMA ICS/NIMS and exercise-design/evaluation practices;
- FHWA tunnel inspection, operations, ventilation, fire/life-safety and drainage concepts;
- CISA cyber-physical / critical-infrastructure exercise concepts.

These references do not automatically create certifications.

External credentials/training remain separate evidence records in the Development Passport.

## 9. Security rule

Do not market the tunnel as a hidden bypass around institutional security.

The network may be visually discreet and unavailable to ordinary users, but its operational model must preserve:
- auditable identity/access;
- emergency accountability;
- safe egress;
- isolation/lockdown;
- maintenance access;
- incident reporting.

"Restricted" is an access-control condition. It is not permission to omit safety systems.
