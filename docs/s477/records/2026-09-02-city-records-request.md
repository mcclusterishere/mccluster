## Request text

Hello Building Department,

I am requesting electronic copies of the complete City property and building record for 477 Broad Street, Bridgeport, Connecticut 06604, including records associated with Shiloh Baptist Church and any prior owner, occupant, permit, project, or address record that corresponds to the same parcel.

This request is for a nonprofit building modernization and existing conditions documentation project. We are trying to establish an accurate architectural and engineering baseline before field measurement and design work.

Please provide, to the extent maintained by the City:

- All architectural plan sets and floor plans
- Building and assessor sketches and dimensional drawings
- Site plans and plot plans
- Permit drawings and plan review sets
- Additions, alterations, renovations, and structural drawings
- Certificates of occupancy and use records
- Fire protection, life safety, egress, and evacuation drawings
- Electrical, mechanical, HVAC, plumbing, and fire alarm plans
- Solar, roof, and electrical service drawings
- Door, entrance, accessibility, and opening schedules or drawings
- Zoning plans or drawings depicting the building or site
- Inspection records that contain measurements, diagrams, or building configuration information
- Historic permit jackets, microfilm, scanned plans, archived drawings, and any other record depicting the interior or exterior geometry of the building
- Any GIS, parcel, survey, or other City-held spatial record associated with the property that is not already publicly downloadable

Please include records predating January 1, 2021 as well as records available through the current Park City system. If responsive material is held by another City department, please forward or route the applicable portion of this request to the department that maintains it and let me know where it was routed.

Electronic delivery is strongly preferred. If any responsive record is too large for email, a download link, shared folder, or other electronic transfer is fine. If fees are expected, please provide an estimate before incurring them.

If any portion is withheld, please provide the nonexempt portions and identify the legal basis for each withholding. If no responsive floor plan exists, I would still appreciate the complete property jacket and any drawings or dimensional records that can help reconstruct the existing building accurately.

Please confirm receipt and provide the City tracking number, if one is assigned.

Thank you,
