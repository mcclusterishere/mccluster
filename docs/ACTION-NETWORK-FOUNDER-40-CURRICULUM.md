# Founder 40 — 13-week cohort deliverables and acquisition curriculum

Status: proposed beta operating plan; not implemented or contracted.

## Cohort arithmetic
Each quarter: one five-role U.S. pod and one five-role international pod = 10 new creators. Four quarters = 40 distinct annual placements, including 8 recording artists (2 per quarter). Founder gets one artist-directed song/feature or commissioned song from each artist per quarter (2 quarterly, 8 annual), subject to written rights terms.

## Per-pod founder deliverables
- Artist: 1 original approved commissioned song OR feature/collaboration on founder track per quarter; local performance filming and agreed promo.
- Producer: 1 original rights-cleared beat or equivalent founder-directed instrumental per quarter, delivered with stems, BPM, key, licensing/split terms. Extra beats optional, not implied.
- Engineer: 1 founder-directed recording or mix session per quarter. For international pod, record the local collaborator and transmit organized stems; founder records locally in U.S. Deliver session files and production notes. Onboard additional artists separately as a platform-growth objective, not an unbounded free studio obligation.
- Videographer: 1 founder-brand shoot monthly per pod (3 quarterly) within feasible local geography; international shoots film local artists, product demonstrations or brand footage rather than require founder travel. Capture usable BTS and transfer organized media.
- Editor: 1 primary founder-brand edit monthly per pod (3 quarterly), plus a minimum of 1 short-form social/BTS derivative weekly when sufficient footage is available; collaborate with videographer and respect revision cap.
- All: participate in up to 3 lightweight weekly actions (production, content publication, community/referral review), not 3 full new professional deliverables. Weekly publishing is contingent on rights-approved assets and scheduled availability.

## Deliverable counts (not unique finished music videos)
- Founder-directed artist songs/features: 2 per quarter, 8 per year.
- Founder-brand videographer shoots: 6 per quarter across both pods, 24 per year.
- Founder-brand primary edited deliverables: 6 per quarter, 24 per year.
- Founder-directed original beats: 2 per quarter, 8 per year.
- Founder-directed recording/mix sessions: 2 per quarter, 8 per year.
- Weekly social/BTS cuts: target 2 per week across both editors, approximately 26 per 13-week quarter, subject to actual available footage and rights. Do not count cutdowns as unique original shoots.
- The collaborative song video is one production package with multiple role assignments and rights credits. Avoid double counting package outputs.

## Recurring weekly rhythm
- Monday CREATE: role-specific work, mentor feedback, asset transfer, brief status.
- Wednesday PUBLISH: approved BTS, process, performance teaser, production insight; creator referral link and campaign attribution.
- Friday CONVERT/REVIEW: approved campaign CTA, fan engagement, link-click/verified-signup/returning-user funnel review.
- Suggested maximum three planned actions per person weekly. Avoid making every role record a new song or professional video weekly.

## 13-week curriculum
W1 Identity, creator portal, signed agreements, baseline metrics and consent.
W2 Referral links, platform navigation, content briefs and rights.
W3 Beat/song ideation, creative briefs and filming plans.
W4 Audio production, local recording and remote stems.
W5 Videography, lighting, framing and organized file delivery.
W6 Editing, short-form hooks, accessibility and captions.
W7 Publishing cadence, calls to action and platform-native posting.
W8 Audience discovery, collaborator tagging and ethical promotion.
W9 Incentives, unique redemption and fraud prevention.
W10 Conversion funnel experiments, link attribution and landing-page improvements.
W11 Community retention, subscriber engagement and repeat visits.
W12 Final founder campaign release, approvals and portfolio handoff.
W13 Reporting, creator graduation, feedback and case-study evidence.

## Acquisition funnel and measurement
Creator-specific referral ID + campaign ID + asset/post ID + source channel. Server-side validate signed attribution tokens, retain consent state, deduplicate canonical user identity and protect privacy. Funnel: impressions (where verifiable) -> link clicks -> visits -> verified new accounts -> follows/subscriptions -> verified returning users (meaningful engagement on >=2 distinct days). Separate creator-to-fan relationships from network-unique subjects. Track country based on appropriately consented analytics and never promise reach or signups.

## Scheduling and constraints
Before each month starts, reserve actual production dates, local studio/filming access, footage dependencies, estimated hours, revision cap and approval windows. Three monthly videographer shoots and edits per pod may exceed stipend economics; validate feasibility and lawful compensation in beta before commercial guarantee. Resolve whether a founder-brand shoot is the same as an artist collaboration shoot on a case-by-case basis; if the brief serves both, count one shoot and multiple credited outputs, not two shoots.

## Beta acceptance
First pilot should validate one U.S. and one international collaborative song pipeline, asset/rights handoff, three publishing weeks, accurate signup/subscription/returning-user attribution, and actual labor hours/cost. External contracts only guarantee tested capacity.
