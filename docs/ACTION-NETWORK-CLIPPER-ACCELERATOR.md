# Action Network — 90-day Clipper accelerator (proposal, not live)

## Cohort economics
- Four consecutive nonoverlapping cohorts annually, five participants each, three months each = 20 new creators/year.
- Maximum allocation: $50 per creator/month × five × 12 months = $3,000/year; includes fan rewards, not $50 personal cash.
- Monthly allocation per creator: $5 onboarding advance; $20 reserved fan-bounty pool; $5 verified mid-month objective; $20 verified final objective. Creator direct earnings up to $30/month; $20 never withdrawable as personal stipend.
- Funding must be reserved and fan rewards settled against verified eligible fan actions. Unused reward funds remain in program ledger and are reconciled; never mark them as creator pay.
- The schedule is not the previous two-$25 cash payment scheme. Do not activate either scheme without approved signed terms and classification/legal review.
- Heath: proposed $30 initial payment is **not yet paid or recorded**. Determine whether this is an exception, advance on direct compensation, or another arrangement; obtain acknowledgment and ledger receipt. Never represent it as a loan without an actual loan agreement.

## Monthly journey
- Week 1: invite, consent, site provisioning, creator social profiles, media permissions, training, analytics baseline; $5 advance after approved agreement.
- Week 2: co-design and activate $20 funded fan-reward campaign with caps and fraud checks.
- Week 3: assess qualified traffic, verified tasks, fan conversions and objective eligibility; $5 objective payment only if earned under signed terms.
- Week 4: final objective assessment and up to $20 performance payment; coaching review and month rollover.
- Month 1: onboarding and baseline; month 2: improvement plan if targets missed; month 3: graduate, continue on paid or subsidized hosting, or exit. Do not forfeit legally earned pay or charge fees without affirmative consent.

## Retention ladder
- Hosting: $5 monthly or $50 yearly prepaid (annual equivalent $4.17/month), keep hosted creator page, basic analytics and music-player access subject to rights/usage policy.
- Creator Pro and Campaign Operator: proposed upsell tiers, pricing TBD based on hosting, media, analytics and support costs.
- 20 graduates retained for 12 full months: $1,200 gross at monthly hosting or $1,000 gross at annual hosting. Actual first calendar-year revenue is staggered by cohort graduation.
- Subsidized/free hosting eligibility must be a published, objective policy, not automatic discrimination by nationality; consider hardship-based scholarships with a clear budget and appeals path.

## Instagram-first qualification and cohort analytics (proposed thresholds)
- Hardline applicant screen: 400,000+ Instagram content views in trailing 30 days, 100,000+ each of last four complete seven-day windows, and 10,000+ Instagram profile visits in trailing 30 days; no follower minimum. Require English-language publishing, real portfolio and evidence of account control. These metrics are counts, not unique people. Verify that account Insights exposes comparable windows before enforcing the weekly rule.
- Admission evidence: applicant profile/account ID, metric type, exact date window, submitted Insights evidence, verification method, reviewer, review timestamp and rejection/appeal reason. Avoid relying on editable screenshots alone where authorized API access exists.
- Creator graduation stretch target: 1,000 **distinct verified returning users** attributed to their creator profile within 90 days. A returning user must have a verified account and meaningful non-automated activity on at least two distinct calendar days. Keep signups, sessions, visits, follows and returning users separate.
- Deduplicate network-wide returning users by stable internal user ID, not by summing creator totals. A single user may be attributed to multiple creators for creator-specific engagement but counted once for platform unique users. 20 creators × 1,000 = 20,000 creator-user relationships, not necessarily 20,000 unique people. Forty creators × 1,000 = 40,000 relationships, with possible overlap.
- Attribution: record creator referral token and first/last touch, campaign/UTM, signup source, verified account, subsequent meaningful activity, and consent. Filter bots, repeated self-visits, suspicious bursts, incentive-only traffic and staff/test accounts. Provide creator-level and platform-level rollups with exact measurement windows.
- Dashboards: applicants see verification and eligibility; accepted creators see weekly traffic funnel (IG views → profile visits → site sessions → verified signups → returning users → engaged followers), target pacing, objective review, fan incentive claims, milestone payout status, and reasons for rejected activity. Control admin sees cohort and role comparisons, cross-creator audience deduplication, campaign funnel and site conversion diagnostics.
- Graduation goal of 1,000 returning users is an ambitious pilot hypothesis, not guaranteed feasible. Recalibrate with cohort baselines and separate creator performance from McCluster page load, signup, attribution or tracking failures.
