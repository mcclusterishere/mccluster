# Action Network — 90-day Clipper accelerator (proposal, not live)

## Cohort economics
- Four consecutive nonoverlapping cohorts annually, five participants each, three months each = 20 new creators/year.
- Maximum allocation: $50 per creator/month × five × 12 months = $3,000/year; includes fan rewards, not $50 personal cash.
- Monthly allocation per creator: $5 onboarding advance; $20 reserved fan-bounty pool; $5 verified mid-month objective; $20 verified final objective. Creator direct earnings up to $30/month; $20 never withdrawable as personal stipend.
- Funding must be reserved and fan rewards settled against verified eligible fan actions. Unused reward funds remain in program ledger and are reconciled; never mark them as creator pay.
- The schedule is not the previous two-$25 cash payment scheme. Do not activate either scheme without approved signed terms and classification/legal review.
- Heath: proposed $30 initial payment is **not yet paid or recorded**. Determine whether this is an exception, advance on direct compensation, or another arrangement; obtain acknowledgment and ledger receipt. Never represent it as a loan without an actual loan agreement.

## Monthly journey
- Week 1: invite, consent, site provisioning, creator social profiles, media permissions, training, analytics baseline; $5 advance after approved agreement.
- Week 2: co-design and activate $20 funded fan-reward campaign with caps and fraud checks.
- Week 3: assess qualified traffic, verified tasks, fan conversions and objective eligibility; $5 objective payment only if earned under signed terms.
- Week 4: final objective assessment and up to $20 performance payment; coaching review and month rollover.
- Month 1: onboarding and baseline; month 2: improvement plan if targets missed; month 3: graduate, continue on paid or subsidized hosting, or exit. Do not forfeit legally earned pay or charge fees without affirmative consent.

## Retention ladder
- Hosting: $5 monthly or $50 yearly prepaid (annual equivalent $4.17/month), keep hosted creator page, basic analytics and music-player access subject to rights/usage policy.
- Creator Pro and Campaign Operator: proposed upsell tiers, pricing TBD based on hosting, media, analytics and support costs.
- 20 graduates retained for 12 full months: $1,200 gross at monthly hosting or $1,000 gross at annual hosting. Actual first calendar-year revenue is staggered by cohort graduation.
- Subsidized/free hosting eligibility must be a published, objective policy, not automatic discrimination by nationality; consider hardship-based scholarships with a clear budget and appeals path.

## Global recruitment
- Pilot candidate markets: South Korea, Japan (high income); Brazil, Mexico (upper middle); Nigeria (lower middle), subject to payout availability, contracts, localization and sanctions compliance.
- Country income group is not a proxy for individual creator value or exchange-rate advantage. Select based on music relevance, content skill, audience authenticity, payment feasibility, internet access and legal compliance.
- Avoid blanket higher targets for lower-income countries. Set baseline-adjusted objectives with documented measurement and appeal.
- Creator sites should be tenant-scoped sub-sites within the existing McCluster ecosystem, reusing auth, music player, Control, analytics, Action Network and clipping infrastructure.

5. Add automated cohort analytics, quality-based traffic metrics and accessible appeals.
