# Free creator onboarding, three-day trial and personalized milestones

Status: product contract + offline calculation prototype. No live onboarding, calendar booking, report-card persistence, or service publication enforcement yet.

## Free creator journey
1. Sign up for a free account. Automatically provision a free draft creator landing page using the existing profile identity and safe template. No cohort selection or paid subscription required. Do not conflate free profile hosting with paid custom-domain or premium publishing entitlements.
2. Require the creator to configure at least one legitimate service with a title, description, price or explicit free consultation, and a working booking/checkout/contact mechanism before declaring the page launch-ready. Drafting remains accessible before service completion. Apply moderation, rights and service eligibility checks.
3. Start a three-consecutive-day trial when instrumentation is active. Measure verified profile views, qualified visits, content publishing, service clicks, inquiries and actual completed sales. Record missing measurement separately from zero activity. No invented baseline.
4. After day three, generate a provisional report card and role-/capacity-aware milestones. Show baseline, measurement provenance, service readiness and recommended weekly targets. Review at 14 and 28 days. The first three days are a trial run, not a reliable forecast.
5. Offer exactly one complimentary 15-minute McCluster consultation per creator. Booking is a separate, authenticated, capacity-limited workflow with unique entitlement, available slots, cancellation and rescheduling rules. Do not promise a slot before real calendar availability is confirmed.
6. During the consultation review consented reach metrics, content examples, service packaging, calls to action, traffic sources and conversion friction. Document mutually agreed action items and follow-up metrics.

## Guardrails
- Milestones are recommendations, not automatic grounds for excluding creators, withholding earnings or guaranteeing sales.
- Personalize by creator role, available hours, starting audience and measured results; no demographic or sensitive profiling.
- All sales and payouts derive from verified payment records; self-reported sales are explicitly labeled.
- Free page hosting and one consultation must have published scope and capacity limits. Do not silently enroll in paid subscriptions.
- Separate Founder 40 production placements and five-influencer paid partnership from universal free onboarding.
- Protect report cards, creator activity, notes and consultation bookings with user/tenant authorization; require consent for third-party social account analysis.

## Implementation acceptance checklist
- [ ] Free signup provisions an editable landing page with zero payment requirements.
- [ ] One service is published and actionable before launch-ready status.
- [ ] Server-authoritative daily metrics cover exactly three consecutive days with clear missing-data handling.
- [ ] Report card persisted with policy version, baseline window and measurement provenance.
- [ ] 14- and 28-day review jobs update recommendations without rewriting historic report cards.
- [ ] One free 15-minute consultation entitlement, unique per creator, calendar availability and idempotent booking.
- [ ] Secure creator and staff views with cross-tenant tests.
- [ ] Accessibility, abuse prevention, privacy, refunds, moderation and operational capacity verified.
