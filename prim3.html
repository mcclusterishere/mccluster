Syllabus

PRIM3 owns the canonical songs, episodes, concept sets and labs. McCluster converts each source unit into three beginner-sized instructional modules and adds missing infrastructure/exam foundations without pretending those additions came from the music.

Lab rule: PRIM3 labs use fictional, local, owned or explicitly authorized systems. Offensive concepts stay sandboxed, defensive, architectural, evidence-based or non-operational.
