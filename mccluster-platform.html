Build your own workforce

Your creator network, powered by ours.

Paid subscribers can organize their own promotional teams from eligible Action Network clippers and promoters. Start with a small campaign, use Founder 20 or Founder 40 as planning presets, or scale within your plan's active-worker and campaign limits. The creator funds each assignment; McCluster supplies the campaign workflow and performance infrastructure.

Founder 20

Plan a creator program with up to 20 annual placements, subject to your subscription's concurrent-worker limits and verified campaign funding.

Founder 40

Plan up to 40 annual placements, with quarterly scheduling and geographic/role-specific recruitment as needed. Capacity is not a promise that workers are available.

Founder capacity presets describe a program plan, not an additional subscription or included payroll. Actual invitation, enrollment, and funded work remain subject to availability, worker consent and authorization.
