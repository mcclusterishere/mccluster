What happens after you apply?

Funded cohort seats are limited, but creator membership is not. With your permission and after rights and moderation review, approved portfolio work can be published on your public profile and discovered through the network. Audio submissions can become eligible for music discovery when the publishing workflow is available. Application details and private contact information are not part of the public portfolio.

Launch status: This is a recruitment landing page linking to a prototype application. Submissions, cohort review, publication, and payment are not yet connected to a live backend. Do not treat completing the demo as a submitted application.
