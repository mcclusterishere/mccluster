Reaction Lab

Experience the song, and optionally take part in a recorded reaction study. Camera effects and research recording are separate choices.

Research participation

With your permission, this page can record your front camera during playback. If you separately choose microphone audio, it may capture your voice and nearby sounds. Your reaction is not shared publicly. This prototype records only on your device and does not upload or store anything on the server.

Recording is optional. You can listen without recording, and stop at any time. Read the privacy notice.

Camera and microphone permission requests happen only after you choose to record. No face recognition or emotion scoring is performed.
