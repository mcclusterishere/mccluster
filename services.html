Websites, photography, video & music

One person runs the whole job · Connecticut and Georgia · travel anywhere at cost

Matthew McCluster builds and runs websites, manages social media, shoots photography and video, and writes original music for brands, churches, nonprofits and small businesses. He works from two home bases, Bridgeport, Connecticut and Decatur, Georgia, and the same person who scopes a job is the one who delivers it. Website design, build and hosting

A custom website on your own domain, built by hand and kept online: the domain registration, hosting, SSL and backups. The build itself is included; you pay for the address and the machine that answers it. A small deposit starts a draft you can preview before buying the domain.

  • You get: a domain for a year, hosting, SSL, backups, and the website build.
  • Work to look at: the Shiloh Baptist Church app and a lead site for Yohana Robertson, realtor; McCluster Sites and the long version of how it works.
  • How it is built: on the same platform described in the platform case study, and this site is an example of the work: fast static pages, accessible markup and structured data.

Website and social media management

Anti-Social runs your website and your social presence as one service: website edits and maintenance, hosting, payment connections, social account management, scheduling, captions and posting, brand voice, and reporting. It comes in two modes: M Mode, the standard monthly price where you keep all of your revenue, and an Equity Uprise mode, a lower monthly price against a capped, time-limited share of eligible connected revenue, subject to approval and a written agreement.

  • Work to look at: reactivating Designer Kicks, a dormant Facebook page, with the numbers before and after.

Photography and video

Who Did the Shoot is photography and video, shot and graded in house: event coverage, portraits and headshots, brand shoots, product photography, interviews, short-form and promotional video, music videos and brand films. Deliverables and usage rights hand over on delivery, in writing.

  • Work to look at: the gallery, and the shoots with their own pages: Candler Crossing groundbreaking for DeKalb County, Dressed for Success with 100 Black Men of South DeKalb, Brunch & Beats for Future Successors of ATL, the Equity Uprise rally in Bridgeport, the Vaunt runway campaign and the I AM HERE and Heal the 3 covers.
  • Film: the lyric videos are shot and cut in house.
  • Kit: Sony A7R V with G Master lenses and a DJI RS 4 Pro gimbal.

Original music, scoring and licensing

Write a Song is an original song built into a campaign rather than a jingle bought off a shelf: songwriting, production, the campaign concept, short-form assets and the rollout. Custom scoring is priced per finished minute, and the catalogue is available for sync licensing for web, film, broadcast and campaigns.

  • Work to look at: the registered catalogue, every track with its ISRC, including the Vaunt Private Flights jingle; I AM HERE, the album.
  • License a track: the licensing desk.

Creative direction and campaigns

The lanes above can run as one engagement: the campaign concept, the shoot, original music or scoring, the web experience and the rollout plan, directed by the same person who makes them.

  • Work to look at: the portfolio, searchable by client, service and year.
  • Pricing: prices are not repeated on this page. The booking page renders the current approved terms from one ledger, so there is only one place a price can be.

How a job runs

  • Four questions on the booking page say which conversation you need.
  • A free call scopes the deliverables, timeline and budget, and whether he is the right person. If he is not, he says so.
  • Production with checkpoints you can see along the way.
  • Delivery: clean finals, rights in writing, and ongoing management on the table for anything that stays live.
