Research program

One live system, two evidence planes.

Real product→ Opportunity + decision ledger→ Audience Science→ Production policy+ Research Lab→ Validated result→ Paper / dataset / software release

The production plane uses only promoted, versioned policies. The Research Lab can run offline replay, shadow decisions, ghost comparisons, low-risk randomized tests and opt-in frontier interfaces without silently becoming the default product.

Observe

First-party events, sessions, music behavior, actions, acquisition, friction and explicit preferences.

Decide

Record every eligible option, selected item, order, policy version, experiment arm and propensity before rendering.

Publish

Archive code, protocols, de-identified datasets, methods and results with versioned research metadata and DOI-ready releases.

Flagship product + commercial research system

CIA Mind Control is not separate from the lab.

The album is the core attention product. The research question is not merely whether people press play; it is how a direct artist-owned experience can turn discovery into deeper catalog listening, a durable fan relationship, participation, distribution and revenue without surrendering the relationship to a third-party platform.

Business-model loop

Discovery→ Album→ Direct identity→ Repeat relationship→ Revenue / action

Monetization tests can include direct support and purchases, music licensing, creator/clip distribution, commerce, services and later subscription/member products. Each test should declare the commercial hypothesis and primary metric before traffic is evaluated.

Questions worth proving

  • Which entry content creates broad catalog exploration rather than single-track consumption?
  • Which experiences turn a listener into a returning direct relationship?
  • Which interventions increase useful revenue without degrading satisfaction or diversity?
  • How does creator/action participation affect music retention and distribution?
  • What is the incremental value of owning the full funnel rather than sending traffic back to a platform?
Research integrity

Product data is not automatically a human-subject research dataset.

Routine product analytics and research intended to produce generalizable knowledge are governed separately. Research-intent experiments carry a review status and cannot be marked live in the research registry unless the appropriate determination is recorded.

An experiment is never described here as IRB-approved or exempt because the developer believes it should be. That determination belongs to the appropriate institutional process.

Institutional path

SCSU + open science

For research conducted under Southern Connecticut State University auspices, the project can be routed through the SCSU IRB/Kuali process and the required human-subjects training. Research outputs can then be versioned from GitHub, archived on Zenodo, assigned DOIs, and added to the lead researcher's ORCID record.

Commercial R&D funding

Research becomes fundable when the technical risk, experiment and commercial path are documented together.

The current NSF SBIR/STTR solicitation supports U.S. small businesses developing high-risk technologies with commercial impact. The Research Lab is being structured so a funding application can point to versioned software, technical hypotheses, experiment protocols, real product evidence and a commercialization path rather than a loose collection of analytics screenshots.
