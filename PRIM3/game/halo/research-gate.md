# PRIM3 Halo Research Gate

Country/site assignments are not canon until this gate is completed.

## Source order

1. Current authoritative PRIM3 character roster and character canon.
2. Current 21-core curriculum/episode mapping behind the 66-mission campaign.
3. Existing Site 0 / Hitman's Halo canon and asset registry.
4. Geography research from authoritative/public sources.

## Required roster fields

- character_id
- canonical_name
- aliases
- canonical role/faction
- country/jurisdiction/region relationship
- relationship type (origin, residence, representation, assignment, affiliation, other)
- confidence
- source references
- known story relationships

Do not infer nationality, ethnicity, allegiance, citizenship, or jurisdiction from appearance or name.

## Country assignment rubric

Score candidate countries against: character-canon fit; learning-core fit; geographic/environmental distinctiveness; architectural/infrastructure opportunities; regional representation across the entire campaign; narrative contrast from adjacent arcs; and the availability of three genuinely distinct mission regions.

## Site rule

Each of a core's three missions must occupy a distinct region/site within the same country and use a fictional facility. Sites should not use the exact identity/location of sensitive real military, intelligence, government, or critical-infrastructure targets.

## Deliverable

The research pass produces a reviewed `world-roster.v1.json` and populated `campaign.v1.json`, each assignment carrying source/canon references and confidence. Null fields in the seed campaign are intentional until then.
