# Mission Location Policy

A PRIM3 Halo location is stored at two resolutions:

1. **Public real geography** — country plus broad region/city/landscape used for the globe and narrative context.
2. **Fictional facility/site** — invented name, coordinates generalized or intentionally offset where appropriate, and explicit `fictional: true` metadata.

The goal is believable global storytelling, not a catalogue of actionable real sensitive sites.

Mission designers should prefer broad public geography and invented compounds such as research annexes, logistics campuses, cultural archives, private labs, industrial test ranges, communications hubs, or fictional infrastructure nodes rather than exact real military/intelligence/critical-infrastructure targets.
