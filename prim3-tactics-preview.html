How this works

Tap a squad member on the board for their actions. Move and Dash light up where they can go; tap a tile to preview the path, then Confirm. Nothing is spent until you confirm.

Get hint is optional: answer a short problem from that member's field (E math, R science, T IT) and the board shows a suggested tile and why. It never moves anyone. Normal play never needs it.

x is the column (0 at the left), y is the row (0 at the top). Grey bars are cover on that side of the tile; dark blocks are walls; the diamond is the objective. Pinch or use + / − to zoom; drag to pan when zoomed.

Progress is saved in this browser.
