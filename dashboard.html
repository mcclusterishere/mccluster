How to actually get one

OneStart a year early The big ones run on a campus endorsement first and a national deadline months later. By the time you see the national date, the real one has passed.
TwoConfirm at the source Every listing here says whether anyone checked it. Unverified means unverified — open the program's own page before you plan around a date.
ThreeWrite the specific thing A fellowship application is not a résumé. It's an argument that you will do a particular piece of work, and that this program is why it becomes possible.
FourAsk the last cohort Most programs publish who got in. They answer email more often than you'd think, and they will tell you what the readers actually cared about.
FiveApply to more than one These are lotteries with a skill component. Three good applications beat one perfect one, and the second is always faster to write than the first.
SixAsk for a person The desk reads drafts. Say so in any conversation on this site and it gets handed to somebody who will.
