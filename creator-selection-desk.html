Creator Selection Desk

Three-day baseline · peer preferences · portfolio evidence · verified Super Creator decisions

Restricted workspace

Sign in through the approved creator workspace to load authorized records.

Peer collaboration preference

Onboarded participants choose who they would collaborate with, not who deserves a public ranking.

Super Creator media review

Restricted to verified Super Creators. Review portfolio media before evaluating team fit.

Final team decision

The decision must be justified, particularly when it differs from peer preferences. Peer identities remain private.

Permissions and safeguards

All submissions require a signed-in session, cohort membership, and a trusted server endpoint. Super Creator verification is hand-selected, never purchased. This interface never writes directly to the database.
