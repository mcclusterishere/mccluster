# Travel place catalog

`cities.json` is a compact city discovery index derived from Natural Earth 5.1.2,
`ne_10m_populated_places_simple.geojson`, downloaded October 17, 2026.

Source: https://github.com/nvkelso/natural-earth-vector/blob/v5.1.2/geojson/ne_10m_populated_places_simple.geojson

Natural Earth data is public domain: https://www.naturalearthdata.com/about/terms-of-use/

The search uses names and approximate city coordinates, not addresses, live
inventory, airports, driving routes or proof of a visitor's current location.
Population is used only to rank matching city names. A manually entered location
has no coordinates until the visitor explicitly chooses a catalog result.
