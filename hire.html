Questions before you book

Is the consultation really free?

Yes. Every call on this page is free — project, partnership, or role. Scoping the work in a real conversation beats charging you to have one.

What does the website itself cost?

Nothing. The build is free. What you pay for is the address and the machine that keeps it online: a $33 domain for the year and $33 a month for hosting. A $33 deposit starts your draft before you buy the domain, and it is credited to that first month rather than charged on top of it.

Who owns the final work?

You do. Deliverables and usage rights hand over on delivery, spelled out in writing before we start.

Do you travel, or work remote?

Both. Based between Connecticut and Georgia, shooting on location; web, strategy and post run remote. Travel is quoted into the project, and shoot work can turn around same-day.
