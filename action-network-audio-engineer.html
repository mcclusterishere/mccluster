90-day paid training pilot · Instagram-first

Become a Audio Engineer in the Action Network.

Record and mix a cohort artist's song and onboard another artist to McCluster's music and website ecosystem.

Application qualifications

  • 400,000+ Instagram content views over the previous 30 days, with at least 100,000 views in each of the four weeks
  • 10,000+ Instagram profile visits over the previous 30 days
  • English-language content and communication
  • Authentic portfolio and ownership-verifiable Instagram Insights
  • No follower minimum.

Proposed pilot eligibility criteria. Views are not unique people. Final acceptance depends on portfolio, collaboration logistics, available seats, and signed program terms.

What you build

Work with a five-person production team to release one song, one video and promotional clips. Artists, videographers and engineers must be colocated; producers and editors may collaborate remotely. You'll get a creator site and training in turning Instagram attention into real, returning users.

90-day platform goal

Stretch target: 1,000 verified returning users attributed to your creator site during the cohort. A returning user has a verified account and meaningful activity on at least two distinct days. This target is experimental and must be validated during the pilot.

Monthly program allocation

$5 onboarding advance + $20 reserved fan incentives + $5 verified objective + $20 final milestone = up to $50 total allocation per month, of which up to $30 is direct creator compensation. Fan incentives cannot be claimed by the issuing creator. Compensation is subject to agreed terms and applicable law.
