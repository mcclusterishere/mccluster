The funnel

Where they came from

Where they go next

The music

Track starts, listeners, repeats, full versus preview plays, completions, shares, album rollups and the underlying media-event mix — all scoped to the same date range selected above.

Signup attribution

Owner-only reconstruction of the seven days before an account was created. Last-touch means the final track heard before signup; assisted tracks are every track heard in that window. This is attribution, not proof that a song caused the signup.

Track → account relationships

Which records are present in account-creation journeys, with listener-to-signup rates where a device bridge exists.

Account journeys

The account, source, last song, elapsed time and observed network/location context used to audit the attribution.

Forensics

Recent owner-only raw telemetry: IP, approximate edge location, ASN/network, device/session and browser context. This section is never exposed to customer analytics properties.
