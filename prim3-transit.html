    How to play

    Tap an operative or a squad card. Move and Dash light the tiles they can reach (stairs count their climb); tap a tile to preview the route and who could shoot you there, then Confirm. Nothing is spent until you confirm.

    Fire lists only known hostiles you have a shot at, with the engine's hit chance. Watch sets overwatch: the first hostile to move into your sight takes a reaction shot. Hunker makes you harder to hit until your next turn.

    Entry can Breach the service gate. Technical must stand next to the relay and Link twice. Then everyone still standing reaches the yellow pad and Extracts. If Technical is lost, the mission fails.

    Coordinates: X grows east, Y grows north; the relay is near (−1, 1). Rules are the Prim3 canonical engine; this page only draws them. Progress is saved in this browser.
