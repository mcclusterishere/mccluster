Insights

Audience

Traffic is who arrived. Audience is who stayed — a session counts as engaged if it converted, saw two pages, or lasted ten seconds.

The funnel

Counted in people, not events, and drawn in one colour because these are nine sizes of the same measure. Accounts come from the auth records, not from the browser, because the browser cannot honestly report one.

Habit

Of everyone active this month, how many came back today. The number a traffic chart can never show.

Where they came from

And whether they were any good. A thousand who bounce lose to forty who stay, so each source carries its engagement beside its size.

Where they go next

What people actually do in sequence, which is almost never what the navigation assumes.

The music

Here the site is the media, so tracks are measured as product: reach, repeat listeners, and plays per listener.
