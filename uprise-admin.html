Topics

Editors can fix wording. Adding, retiring, or re-scoping a topic is the movement's posture, so it is the owner's call and the database refuses anyone else. The listener's own instructions are not editable from any page — they live in the function, on purpose.

People & roles

Owner only. An editor is a fellow with duties: they moderate, they edit listings and topic copy, they read conversations. They never change roles, never send anything outbound, never touch the suppression list, and never delete.

The log
