PRIM3

Music act · Cyber Security Specialist

PRIM3 is an artist of its own, separate from Matthew McCluster: the music act of PRIM3, McCluster Corp's free cybersecurity learning product. Its songs are study tapes, music to build to. After the opener, Got WiFi?, the album follows the PRIM3 course in order.

Managed by Matthew McCluster. PRIM3 does not have a login of its own yet. Until it does, Matthew McCluster's account runs it, the way a label runs an artist.
