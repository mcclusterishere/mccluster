Privacy

What we collect, why, and what we never do with it.

Who holds your data

McCluster Corp. Questions, corrections and deletions: matthew@mccluster.org.

What we collect

  • When you enquire or book: your name, email, and whatever you write to us.
  • When you make an account: your email, and a Google account identifier if you sign in that way. We never receive your Google password.
  • When you pay: Stripe handles the card. We receive the amount, the outcome, and the last four digits — never the full number.
  • When you browse: aggregate page counts. No advertising trackers and no profiles sold to anyone.

Why

To answer you, do the work you asked for, take payment for it, and keep the records a business is required to keep. Nothing else.

Who else sees it

Only the services that make the site work: Stripe for payments, Supabase for accounts and data, Cloudflare and GitHub for hosting, and an email provider for messages we send you. Each sees only what its job needs. We do not sell your data, and we never will.

Client sites

Where McCluster hosts a site for a client business, an enquiry you send that business belongs to that business. Each client's data is kept separate, and no client can see another client's.

How long

Enquiries and project records for as long as we work together, and afterwards for as long as tax and accounting rules require. Ask and we will delete anything we are not obliged to keep.

Your rights

Ask us for a copy of what we hold, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. One email is enough and there is no charge.

Cookies

We use browser storage to keep you signed in and to remember your own settings. No advertising cookies are set.
