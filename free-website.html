What free means here

  • Your account. One free M Account; a handle gives your page its address (front.html?u=you). Your profile must be public for the page to be public; you choose that.
  • Your words and media. Uploads count against the storage allowance shown in your dashboard before you upload. What you upload to a public page is public, even while it is a draft.
  • Save, preview, publish, take down. Drafts are kept with their history. Publishing is a separate, deliberate step; taking a page down keeps your draft.
  • Only you edit it. Another account cannot open or change your page. Building a page does not make anyone a McCluster administrator.
  • Optional, separate. A domain of your own and paid modules are optional purchases with their own terms, shown before any charge. Nothing on this page charges you.

How long a first page takes depends on your media and your words; this page makes no promise about that.
