The page had been quiet for years — averaging 2.9 unique engagements a day across the first two hundred days of 2026. I took it over on July 20. In the fifteen days that followed it did 37,123. Same page, same audience, same product. The only variable was the work.

The shape of it

Two hundred days of flatline is not a slow start — it is a page nobody was running. The line does not bend, it steps, and it steps on the day the posting, the answering and the calendar began. It held above a thousand a day for nine straight days and broke three thousand on seven of them. Fifteen days carried 98.5% of everything the page did all year.

The number I care about most

Across 37,695 engagements and 215 days, exactly one person hid a post. Nobody reported spam. Nobody unliked the page. Volume is easy to buy and easy to fake; volume with no complaints means the reach was earned by things people actually wanted to see. That is the difference between running a page and posting at one.
