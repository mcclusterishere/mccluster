The plain terms

Where The Southern campus, and only that Buildings on the Southern Connecticut State University campus in New Haven. You pick from a list of buildings a runner has agreed to walk to; there is no box to type an address into, on purpose.
When Only while the sign says open There are no posted hours. A run opens when somebody is actually making shakes and closes when they stop or when the night's orders are full. The sign at the top of this page is the only true answer.
Who sees your details The person walking it, and nobody else Your name, phone and room go to whoever is on the run. They are not visible to other customers, and they are not visible to Equity Uprise editors — that is enforced by the database, not by a promise.
If it goes wrong Say so and it gets fixed Wrong shake, cold shake, nobody came: reply to your receipt or text the number on it. Refunds go back to the card Stripe charged.

Run by McCluster Corp. Allergens are listed where we know them — everything is made on the same equipment, so if a nut allergy is serious, this isn't a safe kitchen for you.
